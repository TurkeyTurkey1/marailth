From the Age of Kingdoms to the Age of Nations

The Chronicle

Three thousand years of exploration, trade, rivalry and war — then fifty-five years that redrew everything. The codex gives each reign its own chapter; this is the spine beneath them.

The Oblican Era · Orbon 1080 – 4000
c. Orbon 1003

First contact

Redwickian explorers reach the Plains of Untail and push inland. In the jungles beyond, they meet the Mythali — the original human-like inhabitants of Maradorn.

Orbon 1080

The world map is completed

Humanity now knows Redwick, Cherryfall, Karhiem, Fungladom, Frosthaven, Aluven and Maradorn. Most other lands remain unexplored wilderness.

Orbon 1180

The first Aluvenian government

Maldona, Keldra and Nandarra unite under a single governing council — the first true Aluvenian state.

Orbon 1387

Laperion is discovered

The government of Maldona finds Laperion beyond the western frontier, proving that civilisation exists further west than anyone had mapped.

Orbon 1431

The Sack of Laperion

Laperion is destroyed. Survivors establish Blackborough, and the Laperous Era comes to an end.

Orbon 1900

Maradorn opens its ports

Limited trade begins with Redwick. Azureth and exotic jungle goods leave Maradorn for the first time in quantity. The Mythali remain hidden.

Orbon 2100

The first universities

Maradorn's scholarly orders emerge and knowledge from many nations begins to flow through the city. It becomes known as a centre of learning.

Orbon 2800

The Eastern Maritime League

Redwick, Cherryfall and Karhiem cooperate to protect trade routes — the ancestor of the modern Eastern Island Alliance.

Orbon 3500

The Mythali ruins

Ancient ruins and Mythali structures are rediscovered across Maradorn. Interest in pre-human history grows rapidly.

Orbon 3800

The long decline

Competition over trade, territory and resources sharpens. The peaceful Oblican Age begins to fail.

The Great Diverse War · Orbon 4000 – 4055
Orbon 4000

The fuse

An Aluvenian merchant fleet vanishes in waters claimed by Redwick. Redwick blames pirates; Aluven blames Redwick. Within months, war is declared.

Orbon 4008 – 4025

Expansion

Redwick's coalition — Cherryfall, Karhiem, Frosthaven and Fungladom — faces Aluven, Dunaria, Shadowfen, Ebonwood and Sangar. Trade routes collapse. Entire generations grow up at war.

Orbon 4025 – 4055

The war becomes global

Maradorn is forced into the conflict. Jungle warriors, Frosthaven raiders, Karhiem berserkers, Aluvenian legions, Shadowfen scouts and Ebonwood rangers all fight in the same war.

Orbon 4032

Maradorn chooses a side

Bound by the friendship made when Redwick's explorers first met the Mythali in Orbon 1003, Maradorn supports Redwick — fulfilling an unofficial alliance three thousand years old.

Orbon 4055

No nation wins

Tens of millions are dead. Kingdoms are bankrupt. Cities are ruins and borders are redrawn. A peace treaty is signed, and the modern world begins to take shape around it.

The Pestra-Modgen Era · Orbon 4055 –
After Orbon 4055

The Age of Nations

Nations become more interconnected. International alliances form, modern capitals emerge, and the Eastern Island Alliance grows out of the wartime coalition. Aluven and the Alliance rebuild their armies, waiting for a reason to finish what was started.

Orbon 9973

The Grand Summit

The Council of Marailthian Nations meets — Maradorn, Solvyra, Frosthaven, Fungladom, Ellarion, Aluven, Redwick, Karhiem, Dunaria, Shadowfen, Ebonwood, Sangar and the guild of wizards among them. The summit moves; the arguments travel with it.

The Ascension · a later age
The Ascension

Solvyra rises

Where the skies had been thought empty, an island appears above the seas, encircled by eight islands in perfect formation. Scholars from Maradorn are sent not to claim it, but to understand it.

The Raising of the Banner

The Solvyran flag

After decades without a national symbol, representatives of the eight islands agree on a banner: sky, life, balance, the eagle of Maradorn and the red bird of Solvyra. It belongs to no single god or family.
