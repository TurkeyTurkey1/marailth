Pippy

Pippy is a scarlet macaw and one of the two lifelong companions of Maxorian, Guardian of Maralith. As a young boy exploring the ancient jungles of southern Maralith, Maxorian discovered an injured macaw chick alone after a violent storm had scattered its flock. Refusing to leave the helpless bird behind, he carefully nursed the chick back to health, naming him Pippy. From that day onward, the two became inseparable. As Pippy matured, he formed a rare and powerful connection with Vindr, the Element of Wind. The skies themselves seem to answer his call, allowing him to ride powerful air currents, sense changes in the weather, and fly with extraordinary speed and grace. His brilliant crimson, blue, and golden feathers have become a symbol of hope throughout the kingdoms, for wherever Pippy is seen soaring overhead, the Guardian is never far behind. Cheerful, clever, and endlessly curious, Pippy is always eager to explore new lands, but when danger threatens Maralith, his courage is unwavering. He is not simply Maxorian’s companion—he is family, and together their friendship has become one of the greatest legends of the realm.

Dexter

Dexter is a rare Blue Finco Dragon and the lifelong dragon companion of Maxorian. During the same childhood expedition into the ancient jungles, Maxorian discovered a hidden nest containing a lone Blue Finco hatchling whose parents had disappeared. Choosing compassion over fear, he rescued the young dragon and named him Dexter. As the years passed, Dexter forged a deep bond with Birstang, the Element of Strength, making him one of the most formidable dragons in Maralith. His immense power, sapphire-blue scales, and thunderous wings command respect across every kingdom, yet those who know him best recognise his gentle and peaceful nature. Dexter has little interest in unnecessary conflict and, when Maralith is at peace, can often be found sleeping for days in warm caves, mountain sanctuaries, or beneath the branches of the Elderheart Tree. However, the moment Maxorian calls or the realm is threatened, Dexter awakens without hesitation, ready to defend his friends with unmatched courage and unwavering loyalty. Alongside Maxorian and Pippy, he forms one of the most celebrated friendships in the history of Maralith, proving that true strength is measured not only by power, but also by loyalty, wisdom, and compassion.

Oakley

Oakley is a great bear of the Ashwood Frontier and the lifelong companion of the explorer Marius Thornvale. As a young cub, Oakley was discovered trapped beneath a fallen tree deep within the dry woodlands, during Marius’s time mapping the uncharted western borderlands of Aluven. After being rescued and cared for back to health, Oakley chose to remain by Marius’s side, forming a bond that would shape both of their lives. Over time, the pair grew inseparable, living quietly within the frontier where they built a home beneath a towering ancient oak tree.

As Oakley matured, he became a powerful yet gentle guardian of the wilderness. Though his strength is immense and capable of fending off any threat to their home, he is known for his calm nature and deep connection to the land. He spends his days roaming the forests, fishing in rivers, and exploring ruins alongside Marius, protecting the Ashwood Frontier not through conquest, but through presence and balance. Together, Marius and Oakley are rarely seen, but their legend persists among travellers who speak of a great bear and a lone woodsman watching over the forgotten wilds. In the history of Maralith, Oakley is remembered as a symbol of harmony between civilisation and nature, and one of the most enduring bonds of friendship in the realm.

Bailey

Bailey is a small golden Labrador and a beloved companion of the Maxorian family. Unlike the great beasts and legendary creatures of Maralith, Bailey’s strength lies in her warmth, loyalty, and the comfort she brings to those around her. She was first brought into the family home during Maxorian’s early childhood, quickly becoming a constant source of joy, companionship, and stability throughout his life. Gentle and affectionate, Bailey is known throughout the household for her love of food, her devotion to family, and her instinct to bring comfort to anyone in need of it.

Though she appears ordinary compared to dragons or elemental beasts, Bailey is deeply connected to Deloi, the Element of Life and Bonds. This connection is subtle but powerful, allowing her presence to calm sorrow, ease tension, and strengthen emotional ties between those she stays close to. Many within the Maxorian family believe that her spirit reflects the heart of Maralith itself—quiet, nurturing, and unbreakably loyal.

Bailey rarely leaves the family’s side, preferring to rest near hearths, gardens, and places where people gather. In times of hardship, she is often the first to sense distress, offering silent comfort simply by being present. Within the history of Maralith, Bailey is remembered not for battles or great journeys, but for something far rarer: a bond of unconditional love that anchored the Guardian’s life to home, family, and belonging.

Bo

Bo is a great Bigfoot who lives deep within the uncharted wilderness known as the Gloam Ridge Expanse, a vast and mist-shrouded borderland that stretches between Shadowfen and Ebonwood. This forgotten region is filled with dense, shifting forests, pale stone ridges that catch faint light through the fog, and ancient ruins half-swallowed by roots and shadow. Few who enter the Gleam Ridge Expanse ever map it twice the same way, as paths seem to change and vanish beneath the canopy.

Bo has lived here alone for most of his life, becoming part of the land itself rather than merely inhabiting it. Though his towering size and strength make him an imposing figure, he is known in scattered frontier tales as a gentle giant who avoids conflict and watches over the wild from afar. He does not seek attention or recognition, instead moving quietly through the forests, protecting the balance of the wilderness.

During a time of great danger, when threats from corrupted creatures and violent raiders spread toward nearby settlements, Bo emerged from the depths of the Gloam Ridge Expanse. Without speaking or seeking reward, he defended the frontier villages, his presence alone enough to scatter invading forces and restore safety to the region. Once peace returned, he vanished back into the misty forests, leaving behind only footprints in stone dust and broken fog.

Bo is said to carry a deep connection to Birstang, the Element of Strength, though he expresses it not through destruction, but through restraint, endurance, and protection. In the present day, travellers still speak of a towering silhouette moving between the ridges at dusk, watching silently over the borderlands. Whether myth or truth, Bo remains one of the most enduring guardians of the Gloam Ridge Expanse.

Flinder

Flinder is a hyacinth macaw and one of the greatest explorers and cartographers in the history of Maralith. Living primarily in Maradorn, he became fascinated from a young age with the lands beyond its borders, often circling the great mountains, rivers, and coastlines long before they were fully understood or mapped. Unlike most who remain within the safety of known settlements, Flinder chose a life of constant travel, documenting the world as he saw it rather than as others assumed it to be.

With brilliant blue feathers that mirror the skies above Maradorn, Flinder is often seen gliding over vast landscapes, tracing rivers from their source to the sea and marking hidden paths through jungles, deserts, and mountain passes. His connection to Vindr, the Element of Wind, is believed to guide his instinct for navigation, allowing him to sense shifts in air currents and weather patterns that others would miss. Because of this, his maps are considered the most accurate in all of Maralith, often revealing routes and regions unknown even to official kingdoms.

Though rarely staying in one place for long, Flinder is known to leave behind carefully drawn maps, etched onto parchment or carved into stone, for travellers who might follow in his path. In Maradorn, he is remembered not as a settled citizen, but as a wandering mind who gave shape to the unknown. To this day, explorers speak of seeing a blue macaw gliding across distant horizons, as if the sky itself is still being charted by Flinder’s endless journey.

Moon

Moon is a rare black cockatoo and one of the most energetic and unpredictable companions in Maralith. Living primarily across Maradorn, he is instantly recognisable for his dark feathers, loud personality, and constant sense of mischief. Unlike many creatures associated with wind and sky, Moon is not calm or distant—instead, he is playful, noisy, and always at the centre of attention.

Moon is known throughout Maradorn for his love of pranks and humour. He mimics voices, interrupts serious meetings with perfect impressions, steals small shiny objects just to hide them in strange places, and often appears at the worst possible moment purely to cause chaos. Despite this, he is deeply loved by those who know him, as his antics bring life and energy even to the most serious journeys or discussions.

He shares a strong connection to Vindr, the Element of Wind, though he expresses it in a wild and uncontrolled way. Where others use wind for guidance or travel, Moon seems to become part of it—darting unpredictably through the skies, vanishing mid-flight, and reappearing exactly when least expected. Many believe the wind itself “laughs” through him, carrying his chaotic spirit across the world.

Though he is rarely serious, Moon has an instinct for timing that is almost uncanny. In moments of danger, his pranks can accidentally warn or protect others, and his sudden appearances often change the outcome of events in unexpected ways. In Maralith, Moon is remembered as both a trickster and a blessing—a living reminder that even chaos has its place in the balance of the world.

CN

CN is a large brown bear known across Maralith for his calm presence and his unmistakable blue sweater, which he has worn for as long as anyone can remember. He lives in the remote and rugged region of Karhiem, a cold, forested wilderness of deep valleys, frozen rivers, and scattered traveller routes that connect distant settlements. Few dwell permanently in Karhiem, making CN one of the most familiar and reliable figures for those who pass through its harsh landscape.

Unlike many legendary beings, CN does not seek isolation or attention. Instead, he exists quietly between wilderness and civilisation, appearing wherever help is needed most. Travellers often tell stories of encountering him along broken paths or collapsed bridges, silently lifting heavy loads, repairing damaged supplies, or guiding lost groups back to safety without ever asking for reward. Though he rarely speaks, his actions are steady and purposeful, and his presence is widely trusted throughout the region.

CN is strongly connected to Birstang, the Element of Strength, though his expression of it is grounded in patience, endurance, and support rather than aggression. Many believe that Karhiem itself feels more stable where he walks, as if the land responds to his calm nature. His blue sweater, gifted by an unknown traveller during a harsh winter long ago, has become a symbol of safety in the region—seen as a sign that assistance is near when conditions become dangerous.

In the history of Maralith, CN is remembered as a quiet protector of travellers and workers alike, a gentle force of stability in one of the harshest regions of the world. He does not fight for glory or lead armies, but instead carries the burdens others cannot, ensuring that even in Karhiem’s coldest reaches, no one truly walks alone.

Winter

Winter is a rare frost dragon of the species known as a Glaceryn, ancient dragons born within the frozen skies above Frosthaven. The Glaceryn are known for their pale crystalline scales, breath that can freeze moisture in the air itself, and wings that shimmer like drifting snow under moonlight. Among them, Winter is one of the most trusted and recognised throughout the northern realms.

He resides in Frosthaven, the icy northern stronghold of Maralith, where glaciers, frozen seas, and towering ice cliffs shape the landscape. Winter serves as the loyal companion of the President of Skarn, the governing leader of the Frosthaven territories. Together, the two are deeply connected through their shared bond with Adurna, the Element of Water, which in Frosthaven manifests as ice, snow, and the living flow beneath frozen surfaces.

Unlike many dragons of legend, Winter is disciplined, calm, and deeply protective rather than aggressive. He patrols the skies above Frosthaven, ensuring safe passage through blizzards and watching over the frozen trade routes that connect northern settlements. His presence alone is said to still storms, as if the winds themselves recognise his authority over the frozen skies.

The bond between Winter and the President of Skarn is one of absolute trust. In times of crisis, they act as one—Winter in the air, and the President guiding the land below—maintaining balance across the harsh northern expanse. Together, they are seen as living symbols of endurance and control over the most unforgiving element in Maralith.

In the history of Maralith, Winter is remembered as more than a guardian of Frosthaven. He is the embodiment of stillness within chaos, a dragon whose power lies not in destruction, but in maintaining order across the frozen world.

Peanut

Peanut is a golden lion who lives across the sunlit plains and scattered settlements of Aluven, where wide grasslands, warm winds, and open horizons shape the land. Despite his powerful appearance, Peanut is known throughout the region for his gentle nature, friendly spirit, and deep connection to community life. He is often found near villages, travelling caravans, and gathering places where food, stories, and laughter are shared.

Peanut is strongly associated with Deloi, the Element of Life and Bonds, which manifests through his ability to bring comfort, unity, and warmth to those around him. His presence is said to ease tension between travellers and strengthen the sense of trust within groups who may otherwise be strangers. Because of this, many in Aluven view him not as a fearsome predator, but as a guardian of peace and togetherness.

He is especially known for his love of food and hospitality. Peanut often appears near farms, markets, and travelling feasts, where he is welcomed as both a companion and a symbol of abundance. Farmers and traders alike consider his presence a blessing, believing that where Peanut walks, harvests are strong and journeys are safe.

Though he is capable of great strength, Peanut rarely engages in conflict unless absolutely necessary. Instead, he focuses on protecting others through presence rather than force, standing between danger and those who cannot defend themselves. In times of crisis, his courage becomes unmistakable, and those who underestimate him quickly learn that his gentleness does not mean weakness.

In the history of Maralith, Peanut is remembered as a living symbol of warmth and unity in Aluven—a golden guardian who proves that true leadership can come through kindness, connection, and the simple act of bringing people together.

Brucey

Brucey is a strong and dependable donkey, widely known as the trusted companion of the master of travel routes across Maradorn. Unlike the great beasts and elemental guardians of legend, Brucey’s importance comes from his endurance, reliability, and quiet presence on long and dangerous journeys between kingdoms, settlements, and coastal crossings.

He lives and works around Point of Eloke, a vital travel junction in Maradorn where land routes, caravan paths, and sea crossings meet. As one of the most important connection points in the region, Point of Eloke serves as a gateway for traders, explorers, and messengers moving between distant parts of the world. Brucey is frequently seen carrying supplies, guiding caravans through rough terrain, and patiently waiting at docks and road intersections as travellers prepare for long journeys ahead.

Brucey is deeply bonded to the organiser of Maradorn’s major travel networks, serving as both companion and symbol of safe passage. While he holds no elemental power like many legendary beings of Maralith, he is still quietly associated with Adurna, the Element of Water, due to his constant presence at coastal routes where storms, tides, and sea travel shape the lives of those passing through.

Known for his patience, stubborn resilience, and calm intelligence, Brucey rarely strays from his path or panics under pressure. Travellers often say that as long as Brucey is present, no journey is truly without direction, even in the harshest conditions. Over time, he has become a familiar and comforting figure at Point of Eloke, recognised not for grandeur or legend, but for trust, consistency, and quiet loyalty.

In the history of Maralith, Brucey is remembered as the steady force behind travel itself—the one who ensures that the pathways between kingdoms remain connected, no matter how far they stretch.

Milo

Milo is a rare sea seal known across the waters of Maralith, most often seen along the coasts of Maradorn and the icy shallows near distant trade routes. Unlike ordinary seals, Milo is unusually intelligent and deeply attuned to the rhythms of the ocean, often appearing in places where the sea is shifting, dangerous, or in need of balance.

Milo is strongly connected to Adurna, the Element of Water, allowing him to move effortlessly between calm tides and violent storms as if the ocean itself guides him. Sailors tell stories of Milo appearing beside ships during heavy fog or rough waters, seemingly leading them toward safer currents or calmer seas. Though he does not communicate in words, his presence is widely interpreted as a sign of guidance or warning from the sea.

He is often seen near coastal routes and harbours, sometimes playing in waves, other times resting on floating ice or rocky shores. Milo is especially known to interact peacefully with travellers, occasionally following boats for long distances before vanishing beneath the surface without a trace.

In times of great maritime danger, Milo is said to become more active, appearing in unusual places across the ocean and guiding ships away from disaster. Whether this is instinct, magic, or something deeper connected to Adurna remains unknown.

In the history of Maralith, Milo is remembered as a gentle guardian of the seas—a living symbol of trust between the ocean and those who dare to cross it.

Just as Maradorn is close to being captured, Maxorian returns from the unknown continent and takes control of the defending forces, organising a counterattack and pushing the Aluven army back. However, Maxorian and the boys alone cannot win. The rebuilt expedition fleet returns to Maralith and lands in the remaining free territory of Frosthaven, where the leaders discover the scale of the war. The surviving nations gather their remaining forces, including the Redwick army that has been trapped in occupied territory and manages to break through the Aluven encirclement. Frosthaven sends its army south, while Solvyra contributes soldiers and advanced technology, and the remaining Eastern Island forces join the fight. These forces converge on Maradorn, creating a huge coalition against Aluven. Each group becomes essential to the outcome: Maxorian provides strategic leadership, the five boys use their Mythali training, Maradorn defends its capital, Redwick brings experienced soldiers and knowledge of Aluven tactics, Frosthaven provides fresh forces, Solvyra brings advanced technology, and the returning expedition provides additional soldiers, leaders and technology from the unknown continent. The final battle becomes a massive turning point, with Aluven initially close to victory before the combined forces gradually push them back. The Aluven army finally collapses as its soldiers begin surrendering and retreating, while the dictator is captured rather than killed and eventually faces judgement for his actions. With Aluven defeated, its enormous empire collapses. The victorious nations meet to decide the future of the conquered territories, with Redwick, Frosthaven and the Eastern Islands regaining their lands while Maradorn receives some strategic territory. Aluven survives as a smaller independent nation rather than being completely destroyed. The CMN is rebuilt with stronger systems to prevent another collapse, while the expedition establishes permanent relations with the unknown continent. The five boys, who first met as young prospects at the CMN in 10,000, have now helped save Maralith and become key figures in its future. The story ends with the two worlds beginning peaceful contact, turning the discovery that started the entire story into a new era of exploration, cooperation and friendship.
