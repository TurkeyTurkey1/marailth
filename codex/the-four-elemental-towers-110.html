Book IX · The Elemental Towers

The Four Elemental Towers

Each of the four towers was constructed by the Mythali's in a different sacred region of Maradorn. Their locations were deliberately chosen to reflect the power and influence of the primordial they honour.

The towers are not centres of cities or major settlements. Instead, the Mythali's built them in locations where nature remained dominant.

No permanent human construction is permitted around the towers. Roads leading toward them are deliberately limited, and visitors must travel the final sections of their journey through the natural landscape.

Over many centuries, the jungle has grown around and over parts of the structures. Vines climb their walls, trees surround their foundations and many sections appear almost as though they are being reclaimed by the land.

This is intentional.

The people of Maradorn generally believe that the towers should not be separated from the natural world, as the primordials themselves are responsible for the creation and balance of that world.

The Tower of Birstang

The Tower of Birstang is dedicated to the primordial of Fire.

It stands in sacred land surrounded by dense jungle and is associated with strength, destruction, passion and renewal. Despite being surrounded by vegetation, the tower has survived for thousands of Orbons.

Visitors often leave offerings associated with fire or personal determination. Warriors, guards and individuals preparing for dangerous journeys may visit the tower to seek courage.

Fire is not regarded purely as a destructive force in Maradorn. Birstang represents both destruction and creation: the fire that destroys can also clear the land for new life to grow.

The Tower of Adurna

The Tower of Adurna is dedicated to the primordial of Water.

The tower is located in sacred natural surroundings connected closely to freshwater or the wider movement of water through the landscape.

Water represents life, change, travel and patience.

Travellers, sailors and those undertaking journeys across dangerous waters may visit the tower to honour Adurna and seek safe passage.

Offerings are often placed near the tower rather than directly within water, allowing nature to carry or reclaim them.

The surrounding land remains protected, and no permanent settlement is permitted within the sacred area.

The Tower of Deloi

The Tower of Deloi is dedicated to the primordial of Earth.

This tower is strongly associated with the land itself and is surrounded by some of the oldest natural landscapes in Maradorn.

Deloi represents stability, growth, endurance and the physical world.

Farmers, builders, miners and others whose lives depend directly upon the land often visit the tower.

The structure is frequently described as appearing to grow from the landscape rather than having been placed upon it.

Vegetation surrounds its base, and the tower's isolation is considered an essential part of its sacred nature.

The Tower of Vindr

The Tower of Vindr is dedicated to the primordial of Air.

Vindr is associated with thought, knowledge, movement and the skies.

Birds are particularly significant to the tower, especially the colourful parrots that are common throughout Maradorn. The scarlet macaw, which represents Vindr, is often associated with the sacred area surrounding the tower.

Students, scholars and individuals facing important decisions may visit the Tower of Vindr to reflect or make offerings.

The tower represents the freedom of thought and the ability of knowledge to move beyond physical boundaries.
