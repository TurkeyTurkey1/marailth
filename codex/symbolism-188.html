Book XV · The Plains of Untail

Symbolism

The Plains of Untail hold a unique place in the identity of Maradorn.

They represent:

  • the discovery of the wider world;
  • the history of early exploration;
  • the end of the Great Diverse War;
  • the beginning of international cooperation;
  • the danger of Pyrothas;
  • the sacrifice of the Battle for Maradorn;
  • the rise of Guardian Maxorian.

Few places in Maralith contain so much history within a single landscape.

The Plains of Untail are therefore often described as the place where the history of the known world can be seen in layers.

The ancient explorers crossed them.

The leaders of the nations made peace upon them.

The armies of Pyrothas marched across them.

And the people of Maradorn defended them.

Historical Importance

The Plains of Untail remain one of the most important sites in the known world because they demonstrate both sides of civilisation.

They show that nations can gather, negotiate and choose peace.

But they also show that peace sometimes has to be defended.

For Maradorians, Untail is not simply a field or an old battlefield.

It is a reminder of the responsibility carried by every generation.

Peace was forged at Untail.

Peace was tested at Untail.

And the memory of both remains there.
