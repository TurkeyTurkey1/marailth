2. Political Divisions

Maradorn

The country of Maradorn is the largest and most influential political entity on the continent. It occupies much of the landmass and serves as the continent's leading centre of science, education, philosophy and government.

Maradorn is governed through a unique political system centred around the Guardian and the Council of Elders. Unlike a hereditary monarchy, the position of Guardian is not passed through a family bloodline. Instead, the Council of Elders selects an individual considered worthy of the responsibility.

The Guardian acts as the protector and principal representative of Maradorn. The position requires leadership, intelligence, courage and a demonstrated commitment to the nation. Guardians generally rule for approximately fifty to one hundred Orbons, although individual terms vary depending on circumstances.

The current Guardian is Maxorian, who was selected at the age of sixteen after the previous Guardian was killed during the Battle of Maradorn against Crimsonvale. His selection was based upon abilities and courage demonstrated before and during the conflict.

The political centre of the nation is Mythalindor Castle, which contains the offices of the Guardian and the seven-member Council of Elders. Despite being the head of government, the Guardian does not live the life of a traditional monarch and there is no royal throne. Guardians maintain private lives and families while also carrying out their responsibilities to the nation.

Maradorn is internationally recognised for its highly educated population and unusual scientific development. Institutions such as Maradorn College, Penningsford, the Library of Maradorn and the Maradorn Space Agency have made the country one of the intellectual powers of the world.

Ellariondale

Ellarion is an independent city-state located on the Continent of Maradorn. Although geographically connected to the wider continent and surrounded by the influence of the much larger nation of Maradorn, Ellarion remains politically separate.

The city-state is led by Ella, also known as Ellarion, and maintains its own administration and political identity. Its independence makes it one of the most unusual political entities on the continent, as it exists alongside a much larger and more powerful neighbour while preserving its own sovereignty.

Ellarion shares a long history with the surrounding regions and has cultural connections with the wider Maradorian world. However, it is not governed by the Guardian or Council of Elders and maintains its own internal systems.

The existence of Ellarion demonstrates that the Continent of Maradorn is not politically unified under a single government. Instead, the continent is a collection of different territories and societies, each with its own role and identity.

Despite its smaller size, Ellarion remains an important cultural and political centre and contributes significantly to the diversity of the continent.

Point of Eloke

Point of Eloke is a major territory and regional centre on the Continent of Maradorn. It possesses a distinct identity from both the City of Maradorn and Ellarion and plays an important role in connecting the continent to the wider world.

Its geographical position gives Point of Eloke particular strategic importance. As a coastal territory, it provides access to maritime routes and assists in the movement of people, goods and information between the continent and other nations.

Point of Eloke has developed its own regional character while remaining closely connected to the wider political and cultural sphere of Maradorn. Its importance comes not only from its location but also from its role as a meeting point between the inland civilisation of the continent and the surrounding seas.

The territory therefore forms an important part of the broader continental structure. While the City of Maradorn serves as the political and intellectual heart of the nation, Point of Eloke represents one of the major outward-facing regions of the continent.

Plains of Untail

The Plains of Untail are one of the largest and most historically important geographical regions on the Continent of Maradorn. They are characterised by their broad, open landscapes and form a major contrast to the dense jungle regions found elsewhere on the continent.

Untail holds particular importance in human history because it was among the first areas of the continent reached during the great period of human exploration and expansion. When Redwick sent its major expeditions towards the other cardinal directions, one group of ships eventually reached the Plains of Untail after a long and difficult journey across the sea.

From Untail, human explorers began travelling deeper into the continent. It was this wider expedition that eventually entered the jungles of Maradorn and encountered the Mythali for the first time. The Plains of Untail therefore represent one of the first major points of contact between the human civilisation of the eastern world and the previously unknown lands of southern Maralith.

Over time, Untail became far more than a simple landing point. Its geography made it an important region for travel, settlement and communication. The open terrain provided a contrast to the difficult jungle interior and allowed routes to develop between different parts of the continent.

The Plains of Untail also became internationally important much later in history through the Treaty of Untail, a major agreement that brought together the powers of the known world following one of the most significant periods of conflict in Maralithian history.

The treaty and its location made Untail a symbol of international diplomacy and cooperation. Although the modern international organisation created through the treaty was not named after Untail, the plains themselves remain strongly associated with the idea of nations gathering to settle conflict.

The Plains of Untail are therefore important in several ways:

  • They were among the first parts of the continent reached by human explorers.
  • They acted as an entry point into the deeper lands of Maradorn.
  • They became a major region for settlement and travel.
  • They were associated with the historic Treaty of Untail.
  • They remain an important geographical and cultural part of the continent.

Untail is one of the clearest examples of a place whose historical importance far exceeds its simple geographical role.

Stoomes

Stoomes is a Solvyran-controlled territory located on the Continent of Maradorn. Unlike Maradorn, Ellarion, Point of Eloke and the Plains of Untail, Stoomes is not an independent continental society. It is owned and operated by the technologically advanced nation of Solvyra.

The primary purpose of Stoomes is military and strategic. It acts as Solvyra's major military base on the continent and provides the floating nation with a permanent presence on land.

This is particularly important because Solvyra's main territory is a small floating island with a population of only around three hundred people. Despite its small population, Solvyra is one of the most technologically advanced societies in the world. Stoomes allows it to extend its influence and maintain military capabilities beyond the limited physical space of the floating island.

The territory contains infrastructure associated with defence, military operations and strategic support. It allows Solvyra to protect its interests and maintain a permanent connection with the wider continent.

Stoomes is therefore politically unusual. It is geographically part of the Continent of Maradorn but belongs to another nation entirely. Its presence represents the close but unusual relationship between Maradorn and Solvyra.

Maradorn

The difficult geography of the continent played a major role in its early history. The first human explorers who attempted to travel through the jungles suffered enormous losses due to the harsh terrain, climate and dangers of the wilderness. Of the original expedition, only seventeen survived long enough to encounter the Mythali.
