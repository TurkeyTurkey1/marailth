Book VII · Point of Eloke

Economy

Fishing industry

Fishing is the traditional foundation of Point of Eloke's economy.

The waters surrounding the north-western coast provide a large variety of marine life, allowing the city to support a substantial fishing industry.

Important catches include species used throughout Maradorn and exported internationally.

Fish such as Glispar are among the important resources connected to Maradorian cuisine, while other species are caught specifically for trade.

The city is also a major supplier of:

  • fresh fish;
  • preserved fish;
  • dried seafood;
  • smoked seafood;
  • shellfish;
  • specialised marine products.

Fishing fleets range from small family-owned vessels to larger commercial ships capable of travelling further into the ocean.

Seafood exports

Point of Eloke is one of the primary sources of seafood throughout Maralith.

Seafood produced in Eloke is transported to:

  • the City of Maradorn;
  • other settlements on the southern continent;
  • allied nations;
  • foreign coastal cities.

Because the city's seafood industry has existed for thousands of Orbons, fishing techniques and preservation methods have become highly developed.

The city's exports are particularly valuable to inland nations and regions where access to the ocean is limited.

International trade

Point of Eloke trades a variety of goods in addition to seafood.

Important exports include:

  • fish;
  • seafood;
  • crafted goods;
  • tropical fruit;
  • selected agricultural products;
  • specialised Maradorian products.

The city also imports goods that are difficult to obtain within Maradorn.

Foreign goods arriving through Eloke are transported inland to the capital and other regions of the country.

The city therefore acts as both an export centre and an international gateway.
