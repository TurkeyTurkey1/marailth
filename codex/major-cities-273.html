Book XXI · Aluven

Major Cities

Nandarra and Keldra in particular have historically produced powerful merchants, administrators and political figures.

The government must constantly balance the needs of the major coastal cities with those of the rural interior and frontier settlements.

This balance has often been difficult. Nandarra's merchants may prioritise international trade, while the inland regions may focus on agriculture, security and access to water. Frontier cities such as Maldona must also consider threats and opportunities coming from the less explored regions surrounding Sangar and Ashwood.

Hawker — Capital

Hawker is the modern capital of Aluven and the centre of national government.

Although younger than several of the original settlements, Hawker grew rapidly because of its strategic location and eventually became the political heart of the nation. The city contains government buildings, military headquarters, major educational institutions and residences associated with Aluven's leadership.

Hawker represents modern Aluvenian unity and political strength.

Nandarra — Historic Capital and Trade Centre

Nandarra is one of the oldest human settlements in Aluven and remains one of the greatest trading cities in the known world.

Located directly on the ocean, its enormous harbour has allowed it to become a centre for international trade.

Nandarra's merchants trade with numerous countries and regions, and its markets contain goods from across Drymoria and beyond.

Although Hawker replaced it as the capital, Nandarra remains economically and culturally important.

It also maintains an important trade relationship with Point of Eloke in Maradorn.

Keldra — Second Centre of Trade

Keldra is Aluven's second major trading city.

While Nandarra is traditionally considered the country's largest and most historically important port, Keldra has developed into an equally significant economic centre.

The city has a powerful merchant class and a large population of craftspeople, sailors and traders.

Competition between Keldra and Nandarra has historically driven innovation and economic growth throughout Aluven.

Wanna — The Interior Settlement

Wanna is a smaller city located within the Aluvenian interior.

Surrounded by the country's harsher landscapes, Wanna has developed a reputation for resilience and self-sufficiency.

Its population is more closely connected to agriculture, animal keeping and travel across the interior.

Compared with the enormous ports of Nandarra and Keldra, Wanna represents a quieter and more traditional side of Aluvenian life.

Maldona — The Frontier City

Maldona is located near the borders of the Sangar and Ashwood Frontier regions.

Because of its position, the city has historically been both a centre of trade and an important defensive settlement.

Maldona is particularly significant in wider Maralithian history because its government was responsible for the conviction and attempted execution of Laperous, who later escaped and eventually founded the settlement of Laperion in Ebonwood.

This event eventually contributed to the development of the Laperous Era and the settlement history of Ebonwood.
