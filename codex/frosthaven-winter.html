Winter and the Glaceryn

The Glaceryn are ancient dragons born in the frozen skies above Frosthaven, with pale crystalline scales, wings that shimmer like drifting snow, and breath cold enough to freeze the moisture in the air. They are not numerous, and they are not merely tolerated: in the north they are treated as part of the weather, which is to say as something that must be respected and can occasionally be relied upon.

Winter is the best known of them, and the lifelong companion of the President of Skarn. He patrols the northern skies, escorts convoys through blizzards and watches the frozen trade routes that connect the settlements. His presence is said to still storms, and the pilots who fly the northern routes will change course for no reason at all if they believe he has asked them to.

The bond between Winter and the presidency is understood in Frosthaven as a bond with Adurna, the Primordial of Water, whose element in the north takes the form of ice, snow and the dark water moving under frozen surfaces. Elsewhere in the world Adurna is a god of mystery and defence against evil; in Frosthaven he is the thing that decides whether the road is open.

Frosthaven's histories remember Winter less as a guardian than as an idea: stillness inside chaos, the discipline of doing nothing when doing nothing is the correct thing. It is a very northern kind of heroism, and it is the one the country actually believes in.
