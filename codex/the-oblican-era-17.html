Book III · The Ages of the World

The Oblican Era

Orbon 1080 – 4000

“The Age of Kingdoms”

This is the period where exploration ends and nation-building begins.

Early Oblican Era

Orbon 1080

The world map is considered complete for the first time.

Humanity now knows of:

  • Redwick
  • Cherryfall
  • Karhiem
  • Fungladom
  • Frosthaven
  • Aluven
  • Maradorn

Most other lands remain unexplored wilderness.

Orbon 1120

The first permanent trade route between Redwick and Aluven is established.

Goods begin moving between continents:

  • timber from Redwick
  • fish from Cherryfall
  • furs from Karhiem
  • rare resources from Aluven like gold and other gems that redwick doesn't

have naturaly much of

Orbon 1180

The settlements of Maldona, Keldra and Nandarra unite under a single Aluven governing council.

This became the first true Aluvenian government.

Orbon 1250

The first explorers travel west of Aluven.

Dense forests, swamps and deserts are documented.

The regions later known as Shadowfen, Dunaria and Ebonwood appear on maps for the first time.

Orbon 1387

As you already established:

The government of Maldona discovers Laperion.

This reveals that civilisation exists beyond the western frontier.

Orbon 1431

The Sack of Laperion.

Laperion is destroyed.

Survivors establish Blackborough.

This marks the end of the Laperous Era.
