Blushwood and the Cherry Groves

Blushwood takes its name from the cherry groves that cover its low hills, and it is the oldest settled region of the eastern continent after Redwick itself. The first humans to move inland from the coast went there, and the houses that descend from those families still hold much of the land.

It is a softer country than Dravale: orchards, dye-works, paper mills, and river towns that have grown wealthy on the trade in cherry wood, pigment and preserved fruit. Blushwood is where Redwick keeps its money. Its merchant banks finance voyages that the crown would never dare to fund directly, and its families have a long habit of buying influence in Redwick City rather than inheriting it.

Blushwood is represented at the Council of Maralithian Nations by Rosilia Blushmere, whose house is one of the oldest in the region. The relationship between Blushwood and the crown is polite, profitable and permanently negotiated; the groves have never risen against Redwick, and have never entirely obeyed it either.
