Book XXIII · Wanna

Wanna

Overview

Wanna is a historic inland city located deep within the vast interior of Aluven. Often referred to as the Heart of the Outback, it developed far from the wealthy coastal trading cities and became the agricultural, pastoral and frontier centre of the kingdom. Surrounded by open plains, dry grasslands and rugged wilderness, Wanna has traditionally played a crucial role in feeding and supplying the larger cities of Aluven.

Although considerably smaller than Nandarra, Hawker and Keldra, Wanna holds an importance far greater than its population might suggest. Much of Aluven's grain, livestock and raw materials historically passed through the city or its surrounding districts. Its people developed a reputation for resilience, practicality and strong community spirit, having adapted to the difficult conditions of the Aluvenian interior.

Before the Great War, Wanna was regarded as one of the strongest symbols of traditional Aluvenian life. Following the war, however, the city was transferred from Aluvenian control to Cherryfall, marking one of the most significant territorial changes affecting the interior of the former Aluvenian kingdom.
