Book VI · The City of Maradorn

Economy and trade

The City of Maradorn has a diverse and largely self-sustaining economy.

The surrounding environment provides significant amounts of food, water and natural materials. However, the city also trades extensively with other nations.

Forestry

Maradorn contains vast jungle regions and has access to significant supplies of timber.

However, logging within the natural jungle is highly illegal and carries severe consequences.

Instead, the city maintains logging factories on its eastern side. These facilities use genetically modified trees that grow at an exceptionally rapid rate.

The plantations provide enough timber for domestic use while also allowing Maradorn to export wood to nations with limited forest resources.

This system allows the country to maintain a major timber industry while attempting to protect its natural jungle.

Mining and natural resources

The eastern side of the city contains major industrial, mining and quarrying districts.

Important resources include:

  • cut stone;
  • marble;
  • granite;
  • iron;
  • steel;
  • coal;
  • clay;
  • and other natural materials.

One of the most valuable materials produced by Maradorn is Blosteil.

Blosteil is a form of steel with advanced properties and a distinctive bluish tint. It is particularly valued for use in swords, tools and other specialised equipment.

Because of its importance, Blosteil is only supplied to allied nations.

Azuretie and Azureth tiles

Azuretie is a native blue stone found in the Mythalindor Mountain Range.

It is exceptionally strong, waterproof, fire-resistant and long-lasting.

A refined form of the material is used to produce Azureth tiles, which are required by law for the roofs of houses in Maradorn.

The requirement has become one of the most distinctive features of the city's architecture.

New Azureth tiles possess a strong blue colour. As they age, they gradually develop a green or jade-like appearance.

Older green Azureth roofs are highly respected because they represent age, history and durability.

Azureth tiles are not traded outside Maradorn and are considered an important part of the nation's cultural identity.

Imports

Although largely self-sustaining, Maradorn imports a number of important resources.

Major imports include:

  • salt from Dunaria;
  • oil from Redwick;
  • and various other goods from countries throughout the known world.

Exports

Maradorn exports:

  • sustainably grown timber;
  • selected stone and quarry products;
  • iron and steel;
  • limited quantities of Blosteil to allies;
  • coal;
  • clay;
  • grain;
  • honey;
  • tropical fruit;
  • crafted goods;
  • specialised knowledge and education.

The Point of Eloke also contributes significantly to the wider Maradorian economy through the export of fish, seafood and specialised goods.
