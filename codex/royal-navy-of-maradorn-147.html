Royal Navy of Maradorn

The Royal Navy of Maradorn is the second-largest major military branch and contains approximately 95,000 personnel.

The title “Royal Navy” is historical and traditional. Maradorn itself is not ruled by a royal family.

The Navy protects Maradorian waters and is responsible for defending the country's trade routes across the Great Sea.

Its primary bases are located around Point of Eloke, with additional naval facilities located at other important coastal settlements.

The Navy has developed significantly because of Maradorn's dependence on maritime trade and its long-standing relationships with other nations.

Responsibilities

The Royal Navy is responsible for:

  • protecting trade routes;
  • defending Maradorian territorial waters;
  • escorting merchant vessels;
  • combating piracy;
  • conducting long-distance exploration;
  • transporting military forces;
  • defending coastal settlements;
  • carrying out rescue operations;
  • protecting allied vessels when required by treaty.

Piracy is considered a significant historical concern throughout the known world, and the Maradorian Navy is particularly experienced in locating and intercepting pirate vessels.

The Navy frequently works alongside the Sea Wardens and intelligence services to monitor suspicious activity on the Great Sea.

Naval Philosophy

Maradorian naval commanders generally prioritise manoeuvrability and navigation rather than simply building the largest ships possible.

Maradorian vessels are known for:

  • excellent craftsmanship;
  • reliable construction;
  • skilled navigation;
  • long-distance capability;
  • adaptability during rough weather.

Maradorn's relationship with the ocean is also influenced by its belief in Adurna, the primordial of Water.

Before major voyages, sailors commonly perform small blessings or rituals asking for protection.
