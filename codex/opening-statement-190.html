Book XVI · Kingdom of Solvyra

Opening Statement

For most of recorded history, the skies above the world were believed to be empty—vast, unreachable, and without purpose. That belief ended with the sudden appearance of

Solvyra, a floating island suspended high above the seas, encircled by eight perfectly aligned islands of differing form and nature. This event, known as The Ascension, marked the emergence of the newest land in the world, one that defied all known understanding of nature and balance.

Solvyra was not discovered by wandering explorers, but studied and understood through the efforts of the greatest minds of Maradorn. Philosophers, alchemists, and scholars were sent not to claim the island, but to analyze it, to uncover its structure, its purpose, and the forces that sustained it. Through disciplined observation and experimentation, they revealed that

Solvyra was not a natural formation, but a system, one governed by precise and unforgiving equilibrium.

At the center of this understanding lies the Doctrine of Balance. The eight surrounding islands, each defined by a unique biome and named after a god, contain within their temples powerful artifacts. These artifacts are not symbols of belief alone, but the very mechanisms that hold Solvyra in the sky. Together, they form a perfect distribution of force. Should even one be removed, altered, or misused, the balance would collapse, and the island itself would begin to fall.

From this foundation, the Solvyran people emerged, a society built not on tradition, but on knowledge. Every citizen is trained in advanced disciplines known as Dregions, mastering fields such as philosophy, alchemy, and other high-level studies. Their understanding of the world has allowed them to develop technology unmatched by any other nation, including airships capable of dominating both sky and sea.

Yet this power is not without structure. Solvyra is governed by a strict system of law, designed to maintain order within a civilization that cannot afford instability. Movement, trade, knowledge, and even behavior are regulated to ensure that balance is preserved not only in the physical structure of the island, but within society itself. Above ground, this creates a controlled and highly advanced world. Below, it has given rise to secrecy, hidden networks, and the quiet presence of rebellion.

Despite its position in the sky, Solvyra remains closely allied with Maradorn, maintaining awareness of the world below and its many lands and seas. It is not isolated, but connected both as a symbol of progress and a structure that must be constantly maintained.

For Solvyra is not simply a kingdom.

It is a system of balance, knowledge, and control—held together by eight artifacts and the people who understand them.

And should that balance ever fail, the fall of Solvyra would not be an accident— but the collapse of something that was never meant to be imperfect.
