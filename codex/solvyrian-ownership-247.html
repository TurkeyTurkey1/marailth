Solvyrian Ownership

Solvyra eventually took ownership of Stoomes and incorporated it into its wider sphere of influence.

The decision was strategically important.

Solvyra's homeland is a tiny floating island with a population of only around 300 people. Although its technology is vastly more advanced than that of nearly every other civilisation in Maralith, the physical size of Solvyra makes maintaining a large conventional military force directly on the island difficult.

Stoomes provided the solution.

The mainland city gave Solvyra:

  • access to significantly more land;
  • space for military infrastructure;
  • secure ports and naval facilities;
  • room for aircraft and experimental technology;
  • access to mainland resources;
  • a larger civilian support population;
  • a strategic position near Maradorn and the wider known world.

Following the transfer of ownership, Solvyra invested heavily in the development of the city.

Roads were improved, ports expanded and new infrastructure was built. However, Solvyra did not transform the entire city into a futuristic technological metropolis.

Instead, Stoomes developed in a way that reflected both of its identities.

Much of the civilian city retained the traditional appearance common throughout Maralith, while Solvyrian technology was introduced selectively where it provided significant benefits.

This means that a visitor may see an ordinary stone or timber house, traditional markets and older streets while only a short distance away there may be highly advanced communications systems, military equipment or experimental aerospace technology.
