The Creation of Life

After creating the physical world, the Primordials used their powers to create animals resembling aspects of themselves.

Different creatures were created across the land, water and sky.

According to religious belief, Deloi was especially important in the creation and distribution of land creatures. She created a great family of animals and spread them throughout the world.

Among these creations were two races of humanlike beings:

Humans

Humans are believed to have originated from the Unknown Continents, also known as the Dark Lands.

According to the surviving belief, humans eventually arrived in the known world aboard damaged boats with limited memories of their original homeland.

Over time, much of their knowledge of the Unknown Continents disappeared.

The survivors eventually landed in Redwick, where they developed into the first major human civilisation before spreading throughout the known face of Maralith.

Today, the Dark Lands remain largely unknown, and the original history of humanity has been almost completely lost.

The Mythali's

The Mythali's are believed to have been created as powerful, intelligent and magical humanlike beings.

They were particularly associated with the lands of Maradorn and are believed to have lived there long before permanent human settlement.

The Mythali's constructed many of the ancient structures that remain throughout the deep jungles of Maradorn, including:

  • the four Elemental Towers;
  • the Temple of the Four Primordials;
  • other temples and sacred structures;
  • Mythalindor Castle.

According to legend, the Mythali's have never truly died. Instead, they are believed to remain somewhere within the deep jungles, occasionally bringing good fortune to those in genuine need.

Very little is known about their true history.

Their supposed immortality, advanced intelligence and mysterious disappearance from human civilisation make them one of the greatest mysteries of Maralith.
