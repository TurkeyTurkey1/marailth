Book VI · The City of Maradorn

Food and cuisine

Maradorian cuisine is based heavily around locally available grain, meat, fish, fruit and vegetables.

Staple foods

The most common grain is sungrain, which is frequently baked into bread and served with natural butter.

Common meats include:

  • Rilor;
  • Grivorn;
  • Tarkash;
  • Brallock.

Common fish include:

  • Glispar;
  • Moonfish.

The jungle environment also provides a large variety of vegetables and fruits.

One common leafy vegetable is Unglid, a lettuce-like plant.

Common fruits include:

  • mangoes;
  • pineapples;
  • tropical fruits;
  • summer fruits.

Drinks

Fresh water is supplied by natural springs throughout the mountains.

Children commonly drink fresh fruit juices and sweet drinks made entirely from natural fruit.

Popular warm drinks include:

  • hot cocoa;
  • elderheart tea;
  • warm mint tea, traditionally believed to calm the mind.

Adult drinks generally consist of more sophisticated fermented versions of fruit-based drinks.

The most common is Meldic, a sweet fermented mango drink believed to make life feel bright and happy.

Maradorn does not use alcoholic drinks, drugs or other substances intended to drastically alter the physical or mental state of humans.

Desserts

Popular desserts include:

  • rich cocoa-based desserts containing approximately 90% chocolate;
  • Blushberry tarts;
  • ice cream produced using ingredients and cold resources from the Mythalindor Mountains.

Ellarion is particularly known for producing sweeter chocolate, generally containing approximately 60% cocoa.

Street food

Most street and market food uses the same ingredients as traditional meals but is prepared in forms that can be eaten without the full use of cutlery.

Markets provide an important part of the city's food culture.
