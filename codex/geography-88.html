Book VII · Point of Eloke

Geography

Point of Eloke is located at the north-western peak of the island of Maradorn.

Its coastal position has made it one of the most strategically important locations in the country. The settlement developed around the sea, with much of its history and economy directly connected to fishing and maritime trade.

The town is smaller and more compact than the City of Maradorn. Whereas the capital is relatively spread out and heavily integrated with the surrounding jungle and mountains, Point of Eloke has developed into a denser coastal settlement centred around its harbour, markets and docks.

The surrounding coastline provides access to rich fishing waters, while its position on the north-western point of the island allows ships travelling around Maradorn to use the city as an important stopping point.

The sea is visible throughout much of the settlement and plays a major role in daily life. Fishing boats, merchant vessels and larger trading ships are a constant feature of the harbour.
