Book VIII · Ellariondale

Veterinary education

Ellarion is one of the world's most important centres for veterinary education.

Students interested in animal medicine may train at specialised veterinary institutions before working in sanctuaries, farms, Dragon Keeper facilities or wildlife reserves.

The most advanced students can continue into specialised areas such as:

  • wildlife medicine;
  • creature rehabilitation;
  • magical creature biology;
  • dragon care;
  • surgery;
  • marine animal treatment.

Ellarion's veterinary knowledge is regularly shared with other regions when animals require specialist care.
