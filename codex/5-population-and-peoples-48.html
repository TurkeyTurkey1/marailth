5. Population and Peoples

The Continent of Maradorn is home to several distinct peoples and cultures.

Maradorians

Maradorians form the largest human population on the continent and are concentrated throughout the country of Maradorn.

They are internationally recognised for their emphasis on education, philosophy, mathematics, science and innovation. Maradorian society encourages broad learning, and many citizens pursue knowledge across several disciplines rather than limiting themselves to a single profession.

This culture is reflected in the current Guardian Maxorian, who serves simultaneously as a philosopher, mathematician, physicist, aerospace engineer, author and national leader.

The Mythali

The Mythali are the original human-like inhabitants of Maradorn and remain among the most important peoples in continental history.

Their first recorded encounter with humans occurred during the early Redwickian expedition into the jungle. The Mythali's relationship with humanity later became central to the formation of Maradorian civilisation.

The eventual passing of stewardship from the Mythali to humans led to the creation of the Guardian system, making the Mythali an inseparable part of Maradorian political and cultural history.

Ellarians

The people of Ellarion maintain their own identity associated with their independent city-state.

Although geographically connected to the wider continent, Ellarians possess a separate political identity and maintain independence from the government of Maradorn.

Peoples of the Plains of Untail

The Plains of Untail have historically attracted settlers and travellers due to their accessible landscape and strategic position.

Communities in the region developed in close connection with the wider history of exploration and movement across the continent. Untail's people and settlements have contributed to its reputation as one of the most historically connected regions of Maradorn.

Solvyrians

A small population of Solvyrians is associated with Stoomes, primarily through military and strategic operations.

Their presence is distinct from that of the other continental peoples. Solvyrians come from one of the smallest yet most technologically advanced societies in the world and possess knowledge and technology unavailable to most ordinary people elsewhere.
