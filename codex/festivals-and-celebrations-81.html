Book VI · The City of Maradorn

Festivals and celebrations

Festivals are common throughout Maradorn and are strongly connected to the primordials and the balance of life.

Celebrations do not always occur on fixed dates. Some take place according to:

  • seasons;
  • harvests;
  • military victories;
  • celestial events;
  • and other major occurrences.

Guardian Day

One of the most important celebrations is Guardian Day.

The festival honours the current Guardian and the Guardians of the past.

Celebrations include:

  • feasts;
  • storytelling;
  • competitions;
  • public gatherings.

Elemental celebrations

Each of the four primordials is also honoured through specialised celebrations.

Traditional activities may include:

  • controlled ceremonial fires;
  • releasing birds into the sky;
  • placing offerings into flowing water;
  • and other symbolic rituals.

Festivals are generally colourful, musical and centred around food and community.
