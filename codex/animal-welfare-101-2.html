Book VIII · Ellariondale

Animal welfare

Animal welfare is the defining feature of Ellarion.

The city is home to numerous animal sanctuaries, veterinary facilities and rehabilitation areas. Creatures injured in the surrounding regions are frequently transported to Ellarion for treatment.

The city's veterinary services are considered the most advanced and respected in all of Maralith.

The leader works closely with local administrators, healers, veterinarians and environmental specialists.

The Great Veterinary Clinic

Ellarion contains the largest and most prestigious veterinary clinic in Maralith.

The clinic treats animals from across the known world and is particularly important for creatures suffering from severe injuries.

Animals with serious injuries are commonly referred to the clinic when smaller facilities are unable to provide the necessary treatment.

The institution contains specialists in:

  • surgery;
  • emergency treatment;
  • rehabilitation;
  • exotic creatures;
  • magical creatures;
  • large animals;
  • dragons and other rare beasts;
  • wildlife medicine.

The clinic also works closely with the wider scientific and educational institutions of Maradorn.

Because of its reputation, injured animals may be transported long distances to reach Ellarion.
