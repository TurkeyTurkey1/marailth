1. Arvena — Goddess of Strength

Theme: Strength, Endurance and Resilience

Arvena represents strength and endurance. She is associated with the ability to withstand hardship, survive dangerous circumstances and continue forward despite adversity.

The island of Arvena is dominated by large areas of exposed stone and rocky terrain. The harsh environment represents the idea that strength is not created through comfort, but through the ability to endure difficult conditions.

Solvyrans often associate Arvena with the physical foundations of their civilisation. Buildings, bridges, machines and other structures are designed with the principle of Arvena in mind:

anything worth creating must be strong enough to survive.

The Temple of Arvena

The Temple of Arvena is constructed primarily from enormous stone blocks. Unlike many

Solvyrian structures, the temple contains very little decoration. Its architecture is intentionally simple and imposing.

At the centre of the temple is a massive circular chamber containing the Heartstone.

Artifact: The Heartstone

The Heartstone is a large crystalline stone containing an immense amount of energy.

The Heartstone represents the strength of Arvena and contributes to the physical stability of the Solvyrian system.

The Solvyrans believe that the Heartstone does not simply provide strength to the island—it reinforces the entire Balance.

Religious belief

A common Solvyran teaching associated with Arvena is:

"What cannot endure cannot be eternal."

Followers of Arvena are encouraged to develop resilience rather than simply seeking an easier life.
