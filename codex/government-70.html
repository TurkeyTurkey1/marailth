Book VI · The City of Maradorn

Government

The City of Maradorn is the political capital of the country.

The primary government headquarters is Mythalindor Castle, located near the centre of the city.

The castle contains offices and meeting spaces for the Guardian of Maradorn and the Council of Elders, which consists of seven members.

The castle is not a royal palace in the traditional sense. Maradorn does not operate under a hereditary monarchy, and the Guardian does not rule from a throne.

Instead, the Guardian is selected by the Council of Elders based on their perceived worthiness, ability, knowledge and courage.

A separate Town Hall is responsible for additional civic and city administration.
