Book XXIX · Redwick

The Plains of Dravale

The Plains of Dravale are the breadbasket of the eastern continent and the reason Redwick could afford to become a naval power at all. They are wide, gently rolling and almost entirely without natural defensible ground, which has shaped everything about how they are settled: market towns rather than fortresses, roads rather than walls, and a population used to seeing an army pass through twice a year.

Dravale grows the grain that feeds Redwick City and much of Blushwood, and raises the horses that pull its guns and carry its officers. The great estates are old, and the families that hold them are older; a Dravale harvest is a political event as much as an agricultural one.

The plains are also where Redwickian armies muster. Every campaign the nation has fought in the last thousand Orbons began with the same ritual: the harvest brought in early, the roads cleared, and the levies walking in from the estate towns to a muster field outside Redwick City. Soldiers from Dravale are not considered elite, but they are considered steady, and there are a great many of them.
