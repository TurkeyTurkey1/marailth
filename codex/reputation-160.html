Book XII · The Armed Forces

Reputation

Across the known world, Maradorn is generally regarded as one of the most dangerous nations to fight—not because it possesses the largest army, but because its military combines numbers, education, terrain knowledge, advanced technology and specialised forces.

Foreign commanders are particularly cautious about entering the jungles of Maradorn.

The phrase often associated with Maradorian military strategy is:

“Do not fight the jungle. It has already chosen a side.”

The Armed Forces of Maradorn ultimately reflect the broader philosophy of the country itself. Knowledge is considered a weapon. Education is considered a form of defence. Strength without wisdom is viewed as dangerous, while wisdom without the ability to defend oneself is viewed as incomplete.

For this reason, the Armed Forces of Maradorn remain prepared for war while hoping never to require it.

Guardians of Maradorn (recent of 108 Guardians)

Guardian Maxorian (c. 9066–Present)

Selected at the age of sixteen following the death of the previous Guardian during the Battle of Maradorn against Crimsonvale. Renowned as a philosopher, mathematician, physicist, aerospace engineer, author, cricketer, gymnast and diplomat. Under his leadership, Maradorn entered a new golden age of science and technological advancement.

Guardian Eldric Stormvale (c. 8992–9066)

The Guardian who fell during the Battle of Maradorn. Remembered for his bravery and refusal to abandon the city during Crimsonvale's assault. His death became one of the most significant moments in modern Maradorian history.

Guardian Seraphine Willowheart (c. 8918–8992)

Known as The Peacemaker. She oversaw decades of prosperity and strengthened diplomatic ties with Aluven, Redwick, and Frosthaven.

Guardian Orion Azureth (c. 8844–8918)

A brilliant engineer and architect who expanded Mythalindor Castle and commissioned several of the modern research facilities found throughout Maradorn.

Guardian Lyra Everlight (c. 8760–8844)

Remembered for establishing numerous educational reforms and greatly expanding Maradorn College.

Guardian Thaddeus Ironroot (c. 8685–8760)

A military strategist who modernized the Armed Forces of Maradorn and improved coastal defenses throughout the Great Sea.

Guardian Elyndra Dawnspeaker (c. 8602–8685)

Known for promoting cooperation between humans and Mythali communities and preserving ancient cultural traditions.

Guardian Cassian Starwatcher (c. 8520–8602)

An astronomer and scholar who founded several observatories and inspired centuries of scientific research.

Guardian Rowan Greenmantle (c. 8438–8520)

Focused on environmental preservation and the protection of Maradorn's forests, rivers, and wildlife.

Guardian Aurelia Brightflame (c. 8354–8438)

Led humanitarian efforts following several natural disasters and is remembered as one of the most compassionate Guardians.

Guardian Valorian the Builder (c. 8270–8354)

Oversaw a major period of infrastructure development, road construction, and expansion of the City of Maradorn.

Guardian Arcturus Mythalborn (c. 8185–8270)

One of the most respected Guardians in history. He worked closely with the Mythali and helped preserve many ancient records now housed in the Library of Maradorn.
