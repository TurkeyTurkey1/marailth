Book XII · The Armed Forces

Maradorian Ground Forces

The Maradorian Ground Forces are the largest branch of the military.

They are responsible for defending:

  • the jungles of Maradorn;
  • major cities;
  • mountain ranges;
  • sacred sites;
  • coastal regions;
  • roads and trade routes;
  • strategic natural resources.

Because Maradorn contains dense jungle, open plains, mountains and extensive coastlines, its soldiers are trained to fight in extremely different environments.

A Maradorian infantry soldier may receive training in jungle warfare, mountain survival, urban defence and conventional battlefield combat.

The Ground Forces place heavy importance on small-unit leadership.

Junior officers and experienced soldiers are encouraged to make independent decisions when communication with higher command is impossible.

This is particularly important in the jungles of Maradorn, where difficult terrain and dense vegetation can separate units for long periods.

These are supported by intelligence, engineering, medical, logistics and specialised branches.

Infantry

The standard infantry forms the backbone of the Ground Forces.

Most infantry soldiers are equipped with a mixture of traditional and more advanced equipment.

Maradorn's technological level does not mean that every soldier carries highly advanced modern technology. Much of the military maintains the broader aesthetic and practical technology level of Maralith, while elite units and specialised divisions have access to more advanced equipment.

Standard soldiers may use:

  • swords and Blosteil weapons;
  • bows and advanced projectile weapons;
  • protective armour;
  • specialised jungle equipment;
  • navigation tools;
  • limited firearms or more advanced weapons where appropriate to their unit;
  • communication equipment in higher-priority formations.

This mixture allows the military to remain effective without depending entirely on rare advanced technology.

Mountain Forces

Maradorn's mountain ranges require specialised troops.

Mountain forces are trained to operate in the difficult terrain of the Mythalindor Mountain Range and the Aelthorion Peaks.

Their responsibilities include:

  • protecting mountain passes;
  • search and rescue;
  • guarding sacred sites;
  • defending high-altitude settlements;
  • monitoring dangerous wildlife;
  • supporting religious expeditions where necessary.

They are among the best climbers and survival specialists in the country.

Coastal Defence Forces

Although the Royal Navy protects the surrounding seas, the Ground Forces maintain specialised units for defending the coastline.

These soldiers operate coastal forts, artillery positions and defensive installations near important settlements.

They are particularly important around:

  • the City of Maradorn;
  • Point of Eloke;
  • major ports;
  • strategic islands;
  • important naval routes.
