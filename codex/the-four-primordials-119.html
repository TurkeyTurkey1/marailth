The Four Primordials

Birstang — Primordial of Fire

Birstang is the God and Primordial of Fire.

He represents:

  • courage;
  • fighting spirit;
  • determination;
  • competition;
  • physical strength;
  • sport.

His animal form is the Dragon.

Birstang's dragons can change in appearance according to their emotions. When calm or not angered, they are described as predominantly blue with red highlights, while changes in colour or appearance may represent changing emotional states.

Birstang is commonly honoured before:

  • sporting competitions;
  • battles;
  • difficult physical challenges;
  • dangerous missions.

He is particularly respected by warriors, athletes and those facing situations requiring determination.

Adurna — Primordial of Water

Adurna is the Primordial of Water.

She represents:

  • defence against evil;
  • mystery;
  • the unknown;
  • protection from dark lands and dark people;
  • the oceans and waterways.

Her symbolic animal is the Sea Serpent, although the precise appearance of this creature may differ depending on where Adurna is viewed or represented.

Water is regarded as both protective and mysterious. The sea connects distant lands but also hides dangers and unknown places.

Adurna is therefore commonly honoured by sailors, travellers and those entering unfamiliar or dangerous situations.

Deloi — Goddess of Earth

Deloi is the Goddess and Primordial of Earth.

She represents:

  • family;
  • life;
  • caring;
  • generosity;
  • the land;
  • living creatures.

Her symbolic animal is the Dog, most commonly represented as a golden Labrador, although the breed may change depending on the culture and location in which she is viewed.

According to Primordial belief, Deloi felt generous toward the newly created world. As the goddess of family, she created a great family of land animals and spread them throughout the world.

Deloi is particularly important to families and communities.

One traditional act of respect involves touching one's forehead to the ground, preferably at sacred locations associated with the towers or temples. This act may be performed to bless a family with good fortune.

Vindr — God of Air

Vindr is the God and Primordial of Air.

He represents:

  • intelligence;
  • wisdom;
  • thought;
  • knowledge;
  • cricket.

His symbolic animal is the Parrot, most commonly represented by the Scarlet Macaw, although other forms may be used.

Vindr is particularly important within Maradorian academic and sporting life. Students may honour Vindr before studying, examinations or important intellectual challenges.

The connection between Vindr and cricket is especially significant, making him a commonly honoured figure before matches and competitions.
