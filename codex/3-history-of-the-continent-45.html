3. History of the Continent

The history of the Continent of Maradorn is closely connected to the wider expansion of humanity across the known world.

For centuries, the lands beyond the eastern civilisations were unknown to the human populations of Redwick, Blushwood and Karhiem. During the great period of expansion, ships were sent in several directions to discover new lands.

Around Orbon 1000, Redwick launched a major expedition involving multiple ships. After a long and difficult voyage, the expedition eventually reached what is now known as the Plains of Untail.

The explorers initially established themselves within the open lands of Untail before beginning the far more dangerous journey towards the jungle interior. A group of explorers entered the jungles of Maradorn and travelled for months through intense heat, humidity and difficult terrain.

The journey was catastrophic. Of approximately one hundred and ten people associated with the original group, only seventeen eventually reached a settlement belonging to the Mythali.

The Mythali were the first human-like civilisation encountered by the explorers outside the previously known world. The survivors lived among them for several months and learned that the Mythali were the original inhabitants of the lands of Maradorn.

Eventually, the explorers were escorted back to the coast and allowed to return to the wider human world. This encounter created an early and long-lasting relationship between Maradorn and Redwick.

Over time, human populations became increasingly established across the continent. However, the development of Maradorn differed significantly from that of many other nations because of the continued importance of the Mythali.

At some point during the early history of human settlement, the Mythali passed stewardship of Maradorn to humanity. This event resulted in the establishment of the Guardian system and the selection of the first Guardian, Aelion Mythalis.

From that point onwards, the history of the country of Maradorn became closely associated with the succession of Guardians. Over approximately eight thousand Orbons, around one hundred and eight Guardians have served.

Several of these became legendary figures, including the Guardian who led Maradorn through the Great Diverse War and the current Guardian Maxorian.

The Plains of Untail continued to grow in importance throughout this history. Their position and open landscape made them an important location for meetings between different peoples and nations. Eventually, they became the site associated with the Treaty of Untail, one of the defining political events of the modern world.

In later history, the rise of independent Ellarion added further political diversity to the continent, while the establishment of Stoomes gave Solvyra a permanent strategic position within the region.

The modern history of the continent has increasingly been shaped by science, education and diplomacy. The country of Maradorn has developed into one of the world's leading intellectual societies, while Solvyra's presence through Stoomes has connected the continent to some of the most advanced and secretive technology in existence.
