2. Morak — God of Knowledge

Theme: Knowledge, Discovery and Understanding

Morak represents knowledge and the pursuit of understanding.

Of the Eight Gods, Morak is perhaps the most closely associated with the intellectual culture of Solvyra. The Solvyrans believe that understanding the world is one of the highest forms of respect that can be given to the gods.

This fits directly with the existing Solvyrian emphasis on Dregions, philosophy, alchemy and advanced education. Knowledge became part of their faith rather than being separated from

Artifact: The Infinite Codex

The Temple of Morak

The Temple of Morak resembles a gigantic library and research facility.

Its walls are covered with inscriptions, diagrams and records created by generations of

Solvyran scholars. The temple also contains laboratories where scholars study the artifact and attempt to understand its properties.

Artifact: The Infinite Codex

The Infinite Codex is an unusual device consisting of countless rotating metallic and crystalline sections.

The Codex is believed to contain an enormous amount of information. However, it does not simply provide answers.

Instead, it reveals patterns.

A scholar might ask the Codex about an unexplained phenomenon and receive connections between seemingly unrelated pieces of information.

This has made the Codex one of the most important religious and scientific objects in

Solvyra.

Religious belief

The central teaching of Morak is:

"To understand is to honour."

Solvyrans therefore believe that ignorance is not a sin by itself. Refusing to learn when knowledge is available is considered a much greater failure.
