Book XXI · Aluven

Significance

Hawker is one of the most important cities in the history of Aluven.

It rose from a later settlement into the nation's capital, became the home of the Aluvenian monarchy and government, and eventually found itself transferred to the control of Aluven's greatest historical rival.

Its story reflects the wider history of Drymoria itself: exploration, expansion, rivalry, war and the constant movement of political borders.

For Aluvenians, Hawker remains a reminder of what the nation once controlled and what was lost during the Great War.

For Redwick, it represents one of the greatest territorial and military achievements in its history.

And for the people who still live within its walls, Hawker remains something more complicated than either.

It is a city with two histories, two identities and a single future that continues to be shaped by both Aluven and Redwick.
