Book XV · The Plains of Untail

Plains of Untail

Overview

The Plains of Untail are one of the most historically important regions in all of Maralith. Located within the wider territory of Maradorn, the plains stretch across the eastern reaches of the known world, forming a vast expanse of open grassland beneath an almost uninterrupted horizon. Their landscape is dramatically different from the dense jungles, mountains and coastal settlements that define much of Maradorn. Untail is a land of wide skies, long grass, scattered rivers, rocky rises and seemingly endless fields, making it one of the few places where travellers can see for enormous distances in almost every direction.

Although the Plains of Untail appear peaceful, they have witnessed some of the most important events in the history of Maralith. They were among the first major areas explored by Redwickian expeditions during the expansion of the known world and later became the location of the Treaty of Untail, one of the most important political agreements in Maralithian history. The treaty brought an end to the devastating Great Diverse War and led to the first major gathering of the leaders of the known nations.

The plains are also home to one of the greatest mysteries and greatest dangers in Maralith: the Portal to Pyrothas.

For centuries, the ancient portal stood largely dormant in the eastern plains. Few understood its origins, and even fewer knew what truly existed beyond it. This changed when the armies of Crimsonvale emerged from Pyrothas, transforming the peaceful grasslands into the first major battlefield of the Battle for Maradorn.

Today, the Plains of Untail hold a unique position within Maradorian culture. They are regarded simultaneously as a symbol of peace, unity, sacrifice, danger and resilience. The place where the nations of Maralith once gathered to end one of their greatest wars later became the place where their unity was tested again.
