Book XI · Government of Maradorn

Foreign Affairs

The Guardian generally represents Maradorn during the most important diplomatic meetings.

The current Guardian, Maxorian, is one of the leaders who represents his nation within the wider international political community.

Maradorn's leadership system has historically supported a relatively neutral approach to foreign affairs while maintaining important alliances.

Its most significant long-standing alliance is with Redwick, dating back to the earliest interactions between Redwickian explorers and the Mythali's of Maradorn.

Maradorn also maintains a close relationship with Solvyra, although Solvyra is politically independent and possesses its own leadership system.
