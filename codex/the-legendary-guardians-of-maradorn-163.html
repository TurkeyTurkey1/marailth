The Legendary Guardians of Maradorn

Guardian Aelion Mythalis

The First Guardian

c. Orbon 1015–1092

The first Guardian in Maradorian history.

After the first contact between the Redwick explorers and the Mythali, the Mythali recognized that humanity would eventually become the dominant people of Maradorn. Rather than resist this change, they chose to guide it.

Aelion was a young human scholar who spent years living among the Mythali and learning their customs. When the Mythali formally entrusted the stewardship of Maradorn to humanity, the Council of Elders selected Aelion as the first Guardian.

He established:

  • The Office of the Guardian
  • The first Council Chambers
  • The principles of stewardship and service

He is often called:

"The Bridge Between Peoples"

Guardian Thalor Untail

The Founder of the Plains

c. Orbon 1600–1688

Oversaw the settlement and protection of the Plains of Untail.

Under his leadership, Maradorn expanded beyond the jungle coast and established many of the communities that would later become major towns.

Remembered for:

"Planting the roots of Maradorn."

Guardian Elyria Stormheart

The Defender of the Realm

c. Orbon 2450–2531

Led Maradorn during a period of invasions and piracy throughout the Great Sea.

She reorganized the military and created the earliest version of the Jungle Rangers.

Her victories secured Maradorn's independence for centuries.

Guardian Valerius Dawnforge

The Builder of Mythalindor

c. Orbon 3110–3194

The Guardian responsible for expanding Mythalindor Castle into the great seat of government it is today.

Much of the castle's oldest surviving architecture dates back to his reign.

Guardian Arion Celestaris

The Stargazer

c. Orbon 3620–3708

One of the greatest scientists in Maradorian history.

Founded early astronomical observatories and proposed theories about the stars that would not be proven for thousands of Orbons.

Many consider him the spiritual predecessor of Maxorian.

Guardian Theodric Embervale

Guardian of the Great Diverse War

c. Orbon 3982–4056

One of the most famous Guardians in history.

When the Great Diverse War erupted, Theodric initially sought peace. However, after the pirate conspiracy and the widening conflict threatened the balance of the world, he led Maradorn into the war.

Theodric:

  • Strengthened the alliance with Redwick.
  • Coordinated the defence of Maradorn.
  • Helped organize the coalition against the aggressors.
  • Played a major role in the negotiations that followed.

After the war ended, he attended the signing of the Treaty of Untail.

He is remembered as:

"The Guardian of Unity."

Many historians regard him as the greatest Guardian before Maxorian.

Guardian Selene Brightwater

The Reformer

c. Orbon 5200–5289

Introduced major educational reforms and helped establish the foundations of what would eventually become Penningsford Academy and Maradorn College.

Many credit her with beginning Maradorn's transformation into an intellectual power.

Guardian Orion Skyward

Father of Modern Science

c. Orbon 7420–7498

Established numerous scientific institutions and standardized research practices throughout Maradorn.

Many inventions that shaped the modern world originated during his tenure.

You could even call them The Heroes of Maradorn or The Twelve Legends of Maradorn.

1. Arlen Dawnfire, the Keeper of Hope

The shepherd who kept the sacred fire burning during the Seven Nights of Darkness. He represents courage, hope, and perseverance.

2. Ser Cedric the Unbroken

A knight who defended a mountain pass against an invading army. Though heavily outnumbered, he held the pass long enough for the people of Maradorn to escape. His shield is said to hang in a hall of heroes beneath Mythalindor Castle.

3. Elowen Greenheart

A healer who journeyed across Maradorn during a deadly plague. Legends say she carried seeds blessed by the Primordials and planted them wherever she travelled, bringing life back to villages touched by disease.

4. Thoren Ironhammer

A master blacksmith who forged weapons and tools during Maradorn's earliest years. It is said that some of his finest creations still exist, hidden in ancient vaults and forgotten watchtowers.

5. Lyra Starseeker

A scholar and astronomer who mapped the heavens before modern charts existed. Sailors of the Great Sea still use constellations named after her discoveries.

6. Rowan Greencloak

The first of the Greencloak Rangers, protectors of the wild places. He patrolled forests, roads, and frontiers, helping travellers and defending remote settlements from danger.

7. The Twins of the Sea

Brother and sister sailors, Kael and Mira Tideborn, who are said to have sailed through a storm sent by the gods themselves. Their voyage discovered several islands and trade routes used to this day.

8. Aldric the Builder

The architect responsible for designing some of Maradorn's earliest walls, bridges, and towers. Many builders still offer a prayer to Aldric before beginning a great project.

9. Brynn Riverwalker

A young girl who guided refugees across flooded rivers during a terrible disaster. Though she never sought recognition, her actions saved hundreds of lives.

10. Garrick the Gianthearted

A farmer of enormous strength who defended his village from raiders using nothing but a timber fence post. Over time, stories exaggerated his size until some legends claim he stood as tall as a giant.

11. Marius Thornvale and Oakley

The lost cartographer of the Ashwood Frontier and the bear cub he rescued. Though more recent than many legends, travellers already tell stories about the mysterious woodsman and his faithful companion.

12. Max, the Guardian of Maradorn

The greatest hero of the modern age. Chosen as Guardian of Maradorn, he united the realms in times of crisis, protected the balance of the elements, and ensured that the light of Maradorn endured. While many of the older legends have passed into myth, the deeds of the Guardian are remembered in both history and legend alike.
