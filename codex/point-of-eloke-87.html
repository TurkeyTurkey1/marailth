Book VII · Point of Eloke

Point of Eloke

Overview

Point of Eloke, commonly referred to simply as Eloke, is the second-largest city of Maradorn and one of the most important coastal settlements in the known world. Located on the north-western peak of the island of Maradorn, the city serves as one of the primary maritime gateways into and out of the country. Although considerably smaller than the capital, the City of Maradorn, Point of Eloke possesses an influence far greater than its size due to its strategic location, extensive harbour facilities, fishing industry and international trade connections.

The city is a compact but prosperous port settlement built around its coastline and natural harbour. Its economy is centred primarily on fishing, seafood processing, shipbuilding, maritime trade and the movement of goods between Maradorn and the wider world. Point of Eloke is one of the principal sources of seafood throughout Maralith, with its ships travelling widely and its products reaching countries far beyond the southern continent.

Despite its distance from the capital and its distinct maritime identity, Point of Eloke remains fully part of the country of Maradorn. The city follows the same government, cultural traditions and general laws as the rest of the nation. Its architecture also shares the distinctive Maradorian style, most recognisably through the widespread use of blue Azureth roofs, which give the settlement a strong visual connection to the capital and the wider country.

Point of Eloke is often described as the place where Maradorn meets the world. While the capital represents knowledge, government and ancient history, Eloke represents trade, movement and international connection.
