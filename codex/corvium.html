Book XXXI · The Eastern Islands

Corvium

Corvium was discovered by two people who were not looking for it. In Orbon 4300 a Redwickian ship, sent out in a period when Redwick was short of land and resources, ran aground on a reef in the Floripian Sea. Two of the lowest-ranking people aboard survived the wreck and the open water: Eric Corvale, a mast-watcher, and Maria Turrent, a cook.

They drifted for Lians, then Weils, perhaps an Orbon, desalinating seawater through a filter Eric built out of sand and stone and eating what they could catch. One night they believed they had been struck by a shark. It was land. The place where they came ashore is now the township of Rimaco, at the northern tip of the island.

Eric salvaged timber and light stone from the wreck and built a raft; Maria brought two foxes aboard, Rusty and Sorrel, and gave Sorrel to Eric as a token of trust so that she could stay on the boat. The Corvale family settled, grew, and eventually sent a handful of people back across the sea with word that land had been found. The signal reached Redwick in Orbon 5102 — eight hundred and two Orbons after the ship had sailed, and long after Redwick had recovered the strength to care.

Eric is remembered as the first Emperor of Corvium, and the island has had an emperor ever since. The present emperor, Richard Tator, governs with a senate whose leader holds arguably more practical power than the throne; the resulting system is neither a clean monarchy nor a clean republic, and Corvians defend it fiercely against anyone who points this out.

Goncourt, the capital, is built around the imperial palace and the senate and encircles the old hamlet of Lurix. Its buildings run from two to four storeys, with the occasional single or five-storey outlier, and they are built of whitewashed limestone cut from the quarries north of the island and roofed with gurip, the black water-resistant slate taken from the southern pits. The effect, seen from the sea, is of a city built out of salt and shadow.

Rimaco, on the northernmost tip beside the Floripian Sea, is the military heart of the empire. Some six hundred and fifty thousand soldiers are associated with the city, most of them stationed elsewhere, and its society is organised almost entirely by rank. It is officially part of Corvium and loyal to the emperor, and behaves like a small military nation inside a larger one.

The Lurix family, who held the old hamlet, rebelled against the empire and were bought off rather than crushed: they were granted a small seaside town of their own and the hereditary title of Nōtrucion, the small queen. As a mark of the settlement the town's houses are painted in bright colours, one against another, and roofed in the same black gurip as the capital.

Corvium's culture is loud, competitive and deeply national. Its sport is Stomeball, played four to a side on a court one okt bekavex long, with two points for a full-court shot and one from the main field. Its wildlife includes the volver, a wolf-like creature kept as a pet in its domesticated breeds, and the great deer and bison called tripes and fuson. In the last war it was the only eastern power that chose to stand and fight alone, and it held out for months.
