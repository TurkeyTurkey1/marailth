Book XI · Government of Maradorn

The Council of Elders

The Council of Elders is the oldest and most powerful permanent governing institution in Maradorn.

It consists of seven Elders, each of whom has served for a considerable period of time. Very little is publicly known about how a person becomes an Elder or how the position passes from one individual to another. The origins of the Council itself are also unclear.

This mystery has become an accepted part of Maradorian society. The Council is not a hereditary family, and its members do not appear to gain their positions through public elections. Instead, the method by which new Elders are selected remains largely unknown.

Despite this secrecy, the Council is widely trusted due to its long history of maintaining Maradorn's stability.

The seven Elders are responsible for overseeing the long-term direction of the nation. Their responsibilities include:

  • selecting new Guardians;
  • advising the Guardian;
  • overseeing major national decisions;
  • preserving important laws and traditions;
  • responding to major threats;
  • protecting the long-term stability of Maradorn;
  • maintaining the balance between the different regions and institutions of the country.

The Elders are not intended to control every aspect of daily life. Instead, they focus on decisions that may affect the future of Maradorn as a whole.

Each Elder has an office within Mythalindor Castle, placing them physically and politically close to the Guardian.

The central principle of the Maradorian government is that leadership should be earned through merit, wisdom, courage, ability and service, rather than inherited through birth.

At the centre of the government stands Mythalindor Castle, the ancient former home of the Mythali rulers. The castle now serves as the primary centre of government and contains offices for the Guardian and each of the seven members of the Council of Elders. Despite its importance, the castle is not the centre of every aspect of government, as local affairs are also managed through town halls, councils and regional leaders across Maradorn.

This system has allowed Maradorn to remain remarkably stable across thousands of Orbons, even during periods of war, the deaths of Guardians and major changes in society.
