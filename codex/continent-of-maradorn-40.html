Book IV · The Southern Continent

Continent of Maradorn

Type: Continent Location: Southern region of the known world Major political entities and regions: Maradorn, Ellarion, Point of Eloke, the Plains of Untail and Stoomes Largest political entity: Maradorn Major city: City of Maradorn Notable peoples: Maradorians, Mythali, Ellarians and Solvyrians Political significance: Major centre of knowledge, science, ancient history, diplomacy and culture

The Continent of Maradorn, commonly referred to simply as Maradorn, is a major continent in the southern region of the known world. It is one of the most historically significant landmasses in human history and contains a mixture of ancient civilisation, independent states, advanced scientific societies and strategically important territories.

The continent takes its name from the country of Maradorn, the largest and most influential nation occupying the landmass. However, the continent and the country are distinct entities. Alongside the nation of Maradorn, the continent also contains the independent city-state of Ellarion, the important coastal territory of Point of Eloke, the historically significant Plains of Untail, and Stoomes, a territory owned and operated by Solvyra as a major military base.

Maradorn is particularly notable for the coexistence of dramatically different societies and landscapes. Vast areas of dense jungle and wilderness exist alongside highly educated cities, ancient settlements and some of the most scientifically advanced institutions in the known world. Traditional architecture and lifestyles remain common throughout the continent, while certain institutions and individuals possess technology far beyond that available in most other nations.

The continent also holds a unique position in the history of humanity. It was here that early Redwickian explorers encountered the Mythali, the original human-like inhabitants of the land. The relationship between the Mythali and later human settlers would shape the political, cultural and spiritual foundations of the country of Maradorn for thousands of Orbons.

Today, the continent is regarded as one of the most influential regions of the known world. It is home to the capital of one of the world's leading intellectual powers, an independent city-state, strategically important coastlines, the historically famous Plains of Untail and a foreign military territory belonging to the technologically advanced nation of Solvyra.
