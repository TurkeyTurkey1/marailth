Book V · The Nation of Maradorn

Government and politics

Maradorn is governed through a system centred around the Guardian of Maradorn and the Council of Elders.

Unlike a monarchy, the role of Guardian is not hereditary. Children or relatives of previous Guardians possess no automatic right to succeed them.

When a Guardian dies, retires or is otherwise removed from office, the Council of Elders selects the person considered most worthy of taking the position.

The system is deliberately based on perceived ability, knowledge, leadership and courage rather than family lineage.

The Guardian

The Guardian is the principal leader and protector of Maradorn.

Guardians generally serve for between 50 and 100 Orbons, although the exact duration depends on the Guardian's health, decisions and circumstances. Because humans in Maralith can commonly live to approximately 115 Orbons and leading figures have access to exceptional medical care, some Guardians can remain in office for many decades.

The current Guardian is Maxorian, who was selected following the death of the previous Guardian during the Battle of Maradorn against Crimsonvale.

Maxorian was only 16 Orbons old when selected. His appointment was based on abilities demonstrated before the conflict and his courage and assistance during the war.

The Guardian does not live in the manner of a traditional monarch. There is no throne, royal bloodline or requirement for the Guardian to spend every moment performing ceremonial duties.

Instead, Guardians generally maintain two connected lives:

1. their public life as Guardian and national leader; 2. their personal and intellectual life as an ordinary citizen.

Using Maxorian as an example, the Guardian may have a family and private home while also maintaining an office at the national castle.

Maxorian himself works across several disciplines, including:

  • philosophy;
  • mathematics;
  • physics;
  • aerospace engineering;
  • authorship;
  • and national leadership.

This is considered characteristic of Maradorian society, where highly educated citizens often pursue knowledge across several disciplines.

Council of Elders

The Council of Elders consists of seven members and acts as one of the central governing bodies of Maradorn.

The Council is responsible for selecting Guardians and plays a major role in national government and long-term decision-making.

The origins and appointment methods of the Council's individual members are largely unknown. Each member has typically served for an extended period, and the process through which they originally gained their position is not publicly understood.

The Council operates from the national castle alongside the Guardian.

The secrecy surrounding the Council has made it one of the most mysterious institutions in Maradorian society. Despite this, the Council is generally regarded as an ancient and stable institution.

Government centres

The primary headquarters of government is the castle, which contains offices for the Guardian and each of the seven members of the Council of Elders.

The castle serves as the administrative and political heart of the nation but does not function as a royal palace.

Maradorn also has a Town Hall, which handles additional aspects of government and civic administration.
