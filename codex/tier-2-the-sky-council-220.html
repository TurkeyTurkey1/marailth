TIER 2 — THE SKY COUNCIL

The Sky Council is directly beneath the King. It is responsible for advising the King and managing some of the most important parts of the kingdom, including the military, airships, weather, law and national records.

High Steward

Median Age: 58

Required Qualifications:

●​25+ years of government or administrative experience.
●​Extensive knowledge of law, finance and government.
●​Previous service to the Crown.
●​Strong leadership and organisational skills.

What They Do:

The High Steward manages the King's administration and coordinates the different branches of government. They are one of the King's most trusted advisors and help turn the King's decisions into government policy.

Sky Marshal

Median Age: 55

Required Qualifications:

●​Extensive military experience.
●​Experience commanding large forces.
●​Advanced knowledge of military strategy.
●​Strong leadership and decision-making skills.

What They Do:

The Sky Marshal is the highest-ranking military commander beneath the King. They oversee the kingdom's military forces and advise the King on national defence and military matters.

Generals

Median Age: 47

Required Qualifications:

●​Extensive military experience.
●​Previous command experience.
●​Advanced strategic and logistical knowledge.
●​Successful military leadership.

What They Do:

Generals command large sections of the kingdom's military. They organise soldiers, oversee major military forces and make sure orders from the Sky Marshal are carried out.

Colonels

Median Age: 39

Required Qualifications:

●​Significant military experience.
●​Leadership training.
●​Experience commanding large groups of soldiers.

What They Do:

Colonels command large military units and are responsible for training, organisation, discipline and administration.

Lieutenants

Median Age: 29

Required Qualifications:

●​Several years of military service.
●​Officer training.
●​Leadership experience.
●​Completion of military examinations.

What They Do:

Lieutenants command smaller groups of soldiers and assist Colonels and Generals with managing military units.

Sergeants

Median Age: 32

Required Qualifications:

●​Several years of military experience.
●​Demonstrated leadership ability.
●​Strong knowledge of military procedures.

What They Do:

Sergeants supervise soldiers on a day-to-day basis. They maintain discipline, organise groups of soldiers and make sure orders from officers are followed.

Soldiers / Skyguard

Median Age: 26

Required Qualifications:

●​Physical fitness.
●​Basic military education.
●​Discipline.
●​Completion of Skyguard training.

What They Do:

The Skyguard forms the main body of the kingdom's military. They protect important locations, assist with security and serve under higher-ranking officers.

Airship Captains

Median Age: 41

Required Qualifications:

●​Extensive airship experience.
●​Navigation training.
●​Leadership experience.
●​Knowledge of airship safety and operation.

What They Do:

Airship Captains command individual airships. They are responsible for their crew, the operation of the vessel and ensuring that journeys are completed safely.

Navigators & Crew

Median Age: 30

Required Qualifications:

  • Training in navigation, engineering or airship operations.
  • Practical experience working aboard airships.

What They Do:

Navigators calculate routes and positions while the rest of the crew operate and maintain the airship.

Master of Winds

Median Age: 51

Required Qualifications:

●​Extensive knowledge of weather and atmospheric conditions.
●​Experience with airship navigation.
●​Advanced understanding of wind patterns.

What They Do:

The Master of Winds advises the Sky Council about weather and atmospheric conditions. They help determine when airships can travel safely.

Weather Scholars

Median Age: 38

Required Qualifications:

  • Advanced education in weather and atmospheric science.
  • Knowledge of mathematics and observation.

What They Do:

Weather Scholars study weather patterns and create forecasts for airships, military forces and government officials.

Sky Navigators

Median Age: 34

Required Qualifications:

●​Advanced navigation training.
●​Extensive experience travelling by air.
●​Knowledge of maps and navigation systems.

What They Do:

Sky Navigators calculate airship routes and help crews safely travel between different locations.

High Archivist

Median Age: 61

Required Qualifications:

●​Decades of experience with historical records.
●​Advanced literacy and organisational skills.
●​Knowledge of government documents and history.

What They Do:

The High Archivist controls the kingdom's most important historical and governmental records.

They preserve ancient documents, laws and important knowledge.

High Magistrate

Median Age: 57

Required Qualifications:

●​Advanced legal education.
●​Extensive experience in the judicial system.
●​Strong understanding of kingdom law.

What They Do:

The High Magistrate oversees the highest courts and ensures that the kingdom's laws are correctly interpreted and enforced.
