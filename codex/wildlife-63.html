Wildlife

Maradorn contains one of the most diverse ecosystems in the known world.

Flying creatures

Parrots are among the most important birds in Maradorian culture. They exist in many forms, including scarlet macaws, Hylith macaws, grey parrots, green parrots, pigeons, cockatoos and cockatiels.

Dragons are extremely rare but form one of the most famous parts of Maradorian wildlife.

Approximately 400 dragons have been spotted and documented by humans.

Known species include:

  • the Linco Dragon, a red and orange dragon;
  • the Emerald Dragon, primarily green with golden markings;
  • the Blue-Finco, also known as Birstang's Blue Dragon, one of the rarest documented species.

Approximately thirty-one dragon species have been documented.

Other birds include:

  • Kifling — eagle-like birds of prey;
  • Erulik — large flightless birds resembling a mixture of an emu, ostrich and cassowary;
  • Glimwings — tiny glowing birds associated with and helpful to the Mythali.

Land creatures

Major predators include the panther-like Rilor, six-legged Dongolons, pack-hunting Grivorns, the massive bear-like Tarkash and the rare flame-maned Ashmane.

The Dongolons are historically significant because they attacked early Redwickian settlers on Aluven and were responsible for the deaths of a large portion of the expedition.

Herbivorous and other creatures include:

  • Skaru;
  • Velhorne;
  • Gronth;
  • Brallocks.

Rare or magical creatures include:

  • Feyrills;
  • Glacien Elk;
  • Feyrill Stag;
  • Crysthorn;
  • Sableback;
  • Virex;
  • Rivian Hounds.

Semi-intelligent species such as Korvans and colony-building Thrynnlings are also found throughout Maradorian forests and wilderness.

Aquatic creatures

The rivers, springs and surrounding seas of Maradorn are inhabited by numerous unusual creatures.

Glispar are large river fish hunted by Rilor and some Dongolons.

Thrynn are amphibious lizards associated with water elemental magic and are often found near sacred springs.

Coralyth are small, bioluminescent reef creatures important to magical research and alchemy.

Maridra are large and rarely seen sea serpents believed by some to be connected with Adurna.

Spiralfolk are intelligent orange-shelled creatures resembling nautiluses. They are sometimes referred to as Outsiders and can occasionally be found near water towers and deep ocean regions along the western coast.
