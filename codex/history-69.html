Book VI · The City of Maradorn

History

Mythali settlement

Before human settlement, the region of modern Maradorn was inhabited by the Mythali.

Very little is known about the full history of the Mythali. What is known comes primarily from their interactions with humans and surviving historical accounts. They are believed to live for approximately 180 Orbons, possess exceptional intelligence and pattern-recognition abilities, and may have unusual magical characteristics.

Their population is significantly smaller than that of humans because of their slower reproductive patterns.

The Mythali were the original inhabitants of the region and their ancient civilisation forms the foundation of much of Maradorn's history.

First human discovery

The region was first documented by human explorers from Redwick in Orbon 1003.

These explorers travelled through the difficult jungles of the southern continent and encountered the Mythali. The encounter represented one of the most important moments in the history of human exploration.

The early relationship between the Mythali and the Redwickian explorers eventually contributed to a strong historical connection between Maradorn and Redwick.

Although the first human explorers encountered the Mythali during the Aperion Era, the land was not claimed as Redwickian territory. Instead, the settlement developed independently.

Human settlement

Human settlement began following the Mythali's eventual decision to hand stewardship of the land to humanity.

The main settlement gradually developed as Redwickian migrants and other humans arrived. Over time, the settlement expanded into a major city.

The City of Maradorn was never formally part of Redwick, despite the major role played by Redwickian explorers and migrants in its early history.

Instead, the growing settlement developed its own culture, political institutions, language and identity.

The city continued to grow throughout the following millennia and was shaped by wars, migration, trade, scientific development and the establishment of the Guardian system.

The Great War of Maradorn

One of the most important recent events in the city's history was the Great War of Maradorn.

During the conflict, the city and surrounding nation faced the forces of the warlord Brutak of Crimsonvale.

Crimsonvale is described as the only known town within the hostile landscape of Pyrothas, which is believed to have been created by Birstang during a rage.

The current Guardian, Maxorian, played an important role in the defence of Maradorn during the conflict. His abilities, courage and assistance in the war contributed significantly to his later selection as Guardian at the age of sixteen.
