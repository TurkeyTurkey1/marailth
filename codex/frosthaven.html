Frosthaven

Frosthaven was not founded. It was survived. In Orbon 895 Karhiem sent four battleships north through the Jaybius Channel to look for the suspected land beyond the ice. In Orbon 925 the fleet was lost, its signal gone, and Karhiem wrote off every soul aboard as dead.

They were not dead. Roughly a hundred sailors and civilians came ashore in a frozen country of glaciers, sea ice and cliffs, and they did the only thing that was left: they built. The settlement they raised, Skarn, became the capital of a nation that no one in the world knew existed. They survived the cold, the dark and the fauna, and the generations that followed grew up hard, practical and extremely difficult to dislodge.

For sixty Orbons Frosthaven was alone. In Orbon 985 Skarn built a radio transmitter and sent a signal south to Karhiem — the first word ever carried out of the frozen north. Karhiem did not learn the full extent of the land until Orbon 1080, and the first Frosthaven ships did not cross the northern seas until Orbon 1750, when trade with Karhiem finally began.

The country remains the least documented of the great nations. It is known, it trades, it sends teams to the cricket and its envoys sit at the Council, but most of its interior has never been properly mapped and its settlements keep their own counsel. The land, in a legal sense, still belongs to the descendants of the lost Karhiemian fleet.
