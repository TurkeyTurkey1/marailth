Primordials - there are four of these represented as different animals for each of the elements Air - Parrot (Scarlet Macaw, can change but usually this) (symbolises intelligence, wisdom, cricket) Earth - Dog (golden labrador, changes breed depending where they’re viewed) (symbolises family, life, and caring) Water - Sea Serpent (view changes …) (symbolises mystery unknown and defense from dark lands and dark people) Fire - Dragon (when not angry blue with red highlights, changes with emotion) (symbolises courage, fight, determination and sport) These guys worked together to make the lands and the oceans, the skies and the underworld, Fire got angry and created pyrothas. Using their powers they created the animals to resemble themselves, Earth feeling generous, and being the god of family created a family of land animals and spread them out around the world. These included two races of humanlike animals - The mythali; mythical magical creatures that walked the lands of maradorn especially and the humans who came from the unknown continents; the dark lands, they came on destroyed boats with limited memory, eventually losing their history to time, landing in Redwick they became civilised before traveling to other lands to populate all of Maralith, the dark lands are unknown to anyone on the common face of Maralith. Mythali’s are very intelligent creatures that created the many temples and towers that span over Maradorn's deep jungles, and also made the Mythalindor castle, they are said to have never died giving good luck to those who require it deep in the jungles, see the elemental towers/temple tab. Religion (Maradorn) - Blessing family with good luck has a ceremony to please the earth god - Involves touch head to the ground preferably at certain points throughout towers (forehead) To bless the next day with prosperity in sport/academic or any category a god falls in bless them before you fall asleep the day before. No scheduled “Church” but rather just bless when needed. Other gods rather than the four main primordials God of Fire - Birstang (primordial) (also god of - courage, fight, determination and sport) God of Water - Adurna (primordial) (also god of - defence against evil, mystery and unknown) God of Earth - Deloi (primordial) (also god of - family, life, caring) God of Air - Vindr (primordial) (also god of - wisdom, intelligence and cricket) God of Luck - Lucious (god of all lucky or good decisions and luck, hindsight) God of Risk - Rivian (god of all unlucky or bad decisions and risk, hindsight) Humans traits Lifespan (AVE) - 115 Orbons (about equal to 120 earth years)

Timeline of Maralith -

From HE (Human Era, measured from when humans touched down on redwick)

(measured in Orbon’s (year))

The Eastern Era

Orbon 1 - Humans touched down on redwick, a group of 200 humans evacuated the hellish islands on the unknown continents and after almost 2 Orbon’s of travel 153 human beings landed in modern Fungladom.

Orbon 100 - Human population has grown and so has innovation, they invent the first boats after the great passage, and almost all knowledge of the ancient lands are lost

(now known as the unknown lands)

Orbon 150 - They find modern day Redwick under the King Orlious and began the movement of the human race to The plains of Dravale and The cherry grove of

Blushwood,

Orbon 300 - These civilisations become the first kingdom, Capital of Redwick, with cities of Blushwood and Lylica (now Lywra)

Orbon 500 - More expanse making the map larger, taigas of Karihiem are founded

Orbon 853 - What is known to Karihiem as The great separation or The Original independency in historical context, Karhiem declared independence from Redwick and Plains of Dravale, now a private country.

Orbon 871 - Blushwood follow suit also declaring independence from Redwick

Aperion Era (expansion of the world)

Orbon 872 - the beginning of the Aperion Era or the new expansion

Orbon 890 - Redwick create a large ship, known as The Ranchious (a large battleship, to target the suspected lands across the sea)

Orbon 895 - Karhiem designed another set of battleships (4) to target north in the modern day Jaybius Channel to target the suspected land up north.

Orbon 921 - Redwick lands the Ranchious on the modern day islands of Aluven and claims the settlement with little regard to the indigenous (animals) of the land; it was a very rough journey as the great sea proved very tricky to navigate.

Orbon 923 - The people of the Ranchious get attacked by the natives (Dongolons)

which are creatures, they look like mini-monkeys with 6 legs and spears as their main weapon, they eliminate 50% of the redwickians, or about 100 people the other

~100 manage to escape and begin the multiple different settlements in Aluven (these include Keldra, Wanna, Nandarra (old capital), and Maldona but not Hawker who was settled later and became capital after (like kyoto and tokyo).

Orbon 925 - Karhiem’s boats get destroyed or lost and lose signal to Karhiem and they are deemed dead, these lost humans create the city of Skarn and hence frosthaven, these hundred or so civilians managed to survive in the harsh conditions against fauna. This makes these humans really tough and battle ready for the future.

Orbon 985 - Skarn manages to design a radiologic signal and sends it back to

Karhiem being the first form of communication from the frozen realm of frosthaven.

Orbon 1000 - As a mark for the new millenia Redwick sends off their final mission to the other 3 cardinal directions (south, north, west - as Redwick are east), where a group of 3 battleships lands on the Plains of Untail about a year or so of boat (keep in mind that they aren't travelling in a straight line)

Orbon 1003 - The Redwickians breach the jungles of Maradorn and instantly realise its just very different to the rest of the world, after reluctantly travelling deeper for 8 months of intense hot, humid travelling in the jungle, they reach an encampment (the first human-like creature encampment that has ever been discovered) these

Redwickians (a total of 17 after the other 93 died) learn that these creatures called the Mythali’s are the original inhabitants of the unmapped maradorn.

Orbon 1004 - After living with the Mythali’s for 4 months they get escorted to the docks on the bay and are released with an unfounded respect for this land. This creates an unofficial alliance between Maradorn and Redwick which lasts multiple millennia.

Orbon 1080 - Karhiem learns directly of Frosthaven's land and this makes the modern map fully discovered by this point. (it took Redwick 200 or so years to discover the rest of the world)

The Laperous Era (named after the king of Ebonwood King Laperous the I), And happened after the Aperion Era and during the Oblican Era (or old kingdom - prior to the great diverse (war))

Laperous is like Alexander the great, he was an outcast of Aluven and was planned to be executed by the governing body of Maldona and hence he fled along the border between the desert and Shadowfen, expecting to reach the sea but rather just kept going along land, after 3 Orbons of travel he landed at the coast of modern day ebonwood, and ended up settling there, alone. Eventually on a mission to find food he finds 3 other human beings all exiled or trialed and fled like him and he led them back to what he called Laperion (now practically uninhabitated).

Laperous was convicted at 17

Orbon 1081 - he was convicted and trialed

Orbon 1084 - founded Laperion

Orbon 1088 - Laperion had more people.

Orbon 1100 - Laperion reached 50 people (now known to outcasts to go there)

Orbon 1130 - Laperous became official leader of Laperion

Orbon 1167 - Laperous was brutally mauled by a Pagaer (large grey bear - vicious but stupid)

Orbon 1387 - The government of Maldona learns of the city of Laperion because of the new civilisation, Dunaria.

Orbon 1400 - Blackborough had reached 1000 population through breeding and other outcasts.

Orbon 1431 - the end of the Laperous Era as Laperion gets ransacked by Maldona forces, which leads to the retreat and abandonment of the town and creation of the town of Blackborough, and lots of little taverns and such.

Laperion remains as a deserted ghost town in the middle of the forest.

After a large section of time which travels like the laperion era with leaders coming and going to fully inhabit the modern world we reach the pre-modern era or the Pestra-Modgen era
