Book XVIII · Solvyrian Society

The Flag of Solvyra

For the first several decades following The Ascension, Solvyra had no official national flag.

The people who came to live upon the floating island had originally come from Maradorn, and at first they did not consider themselves to be a completely separate civilisation. They were explorers, scholars and settlers attempting to understand the mysterious land that had appeared above the world. As the population grew and Solvyra developed its own government, technology, education system and culture, the people gradually began to see themselves as something more than simply people from Maradorn living on an unusual island. Their society became increasingly connected to the Doctrine of Balance, the Eight

Gods and the eight surrounding islands, and a distinct Solvyrian identity began to emerge.

The existing history of Solvyra describes this period as one in which the people gradually developed their own culture and understanding of the strange world around them.

As this new identity developed, Solvyrian scholars and government officials began discussing the creation of a national symbol. They wanted something that could represent the entire civilisation rather than a particular city, island, god or political leader. The flag therefore had to represent both the origins of the Solvyran people and the civilisation they had created. After considerable discussion, artists, historians, religious scholars and government representatives were invited to submit designs for a new national banner. Many designs were proposed, but the final design was chosen because it combined the most important ideas of Solvyrian society: the sky in which they lived, the natural world that surrounded them, the importance of balance, their connection to Maradorn and their belief that the future should always be guided by the lessons of the past. The flag eventually became one of the most recognisable symbols of Solvyrian identity.

The light blue background represents the sky. This is perhaps the most obvious symbol on the flag, but it also has a much deeper meaning to the Solvyrans. Unlike most civilisations in

Maralith, Solvyra exists above the world rather than directly upon it. The sky is therefore not simply something that surrounds their civilisation; it is the environment in which their entire society exists. The blue represents freedom, exploration and discovery, while also reminding the Solvyrans of the extraordinary circumstances that created their civilisation. Their position above the world has allowed them to develop in ways that would have been impossible for many other societies, but it has also created a responsibility to understand and protect the forces that keep Solvyra in the sky. The blue field therefore represents both the freedom of the Solvyrans and the enormous responsibility that comes with their unusual home.

Across the flag runs a large green diagonal stripe, bordered by two golden lines. The green stripe represents life, nature, civilisation and progress. The Solvyrans have developed technology far beyond that of many other civilisations in Maralith, but their culture does not consider technology to be separate from the natural world. The eight islands surrounding

Solvyra each possess their own environments and biomes, and these environments became closely connected to the Solvyrian understanding of the Eight Gods. The green therefore represents the living world that exists alongside Solvyran technology. The diagonal shape itself represents movement and progress, rising across the flag to symbolise the development of Solvyra from a mysterious floating island into a highly advanced civilisation.

It is sometimes interpreted by Solvyrian scholars as the path from discovery to understanding, reflecting the importance that Solvyran society places on learning.

The two golden lines surrounding the green stripe represent the Balance. Their position is deliberately symmetrical, with neither line crossing the other or moving away from it. The lines continue together across the entire flag, representing the idea that different forces can remain separate while still working together. This reflects the central principle of the Doctrine of Balance, in which the eight gods and their artifacts each represent different forces that must work together to maintain Solvyra. Gold was chosen because it represents something precious, enduring and worthy of protection. It also represents knowledge, which is considered extremely valuable within Solvyrian society. The Solvyrans believe that learning and understanding the world is one of the ways in which people honour the gods, meaning that knowledge itself is closely connected to their religious beliefs.

In the upper-left corner of the flag is the Solvyrian crest. The crest was designed to represent the Solvyran people rather than any individual god. This was an important decision because the Doctrine of Balance does not place one of the Eight Gods above the others.

Each god represents a different force, and each is necessary for the continuation of Solvyra.

Placing one god on the national flag could therefore have suggested that one part of the

Balance was more important than another. Instead, the crest became a symbol of the people who had been entrusted with maintaining the Balance. It represents Solvyran civilisation, responsibility, unity and the continuation of their society.

At the top of the crest is a golden crown. The crown is not intended to represent a traditional monarchy or the power of a king. Instead, it represents responsibility and stewardship. According to Solvyrian religious belief, the gods created the Balance, but the people were entrusted with protecting it. The crown therefore represents the responsibility that comes with being a Solvyran. It is a reminder that living above the rest of the world does not make the Solvyrans superior to other peoples. Instead, it means that they have been given something unusual and valuable that they must protect. An old interpretation of the crown is captured by the phrase, "To be above is not to rule. It is to protect."

The eagle within the crest represents Maradorn, the ancestral homeland of the Solvyran people. The first Solvyrans came from Maradorn, and this connection remained an important part of their history even after their civilisation became technologically and culturally distinct.

The eagle is therefore a symbol of heritage, origin and the past. It reminds Solvyrans that their civilisation did not simply appear independently in the sky; it grew from people who once lived upon the land below. The eagle also represents exploration because the earliest settlers had to leave their original home and venture into an unknown world. Its presence on the flag ensures that Solvyrian society never completely forgets where its people came from.

The red bird represents Solvyra itself and the civilisation that developed after The

Ascension. Its bright red colour contrasts strongly with the eagle, representing the emergence of something new from the old. The red bird represents independence, innovation, curiosity, advancement and the future. While the eagle represents the origins of the Solvyran people, the red bird represents everything they have built since arriving on

Solvyra. The two birds therefore form an important contrast between the past and the future.

The eagle represents where the people came from, while the red bird represents what they have become and what they hope to become.

The fact that the two birds face each other is especially important. During the creation of the flag, some designers suggested that the birds should face away from one another, representing Solvyra looking outward toward the rest of the world. The final designers rejected this idea because they believed that Solvyra's future should never become disconnected from its history. Instead, the birds were placed facing one another to symbolise the continuing relationship between Maradorn and Solvyra. Neither bird is positioned above the other, meaning that neither the past nor the future is considered more important. The arrangement became associated with the Solvyrian phrase, "The future must remember the past." The eagle looks toward the civilisation from which the Solvyrans originated, while the red bird looks toward the civilisation that they have created.

The green areas behind the crest represent the eight surrounding islands and the natural world that forms an important part of Solvyrian religion. Although the islands are physically separated from the central island of Solvyra, the Solvyrans believe that they are connected through the Balance. Each island possesses its own environment, temple and artifact, and the artifacts work together as part of the system that maintains Solvyra's existence. The green therefore represents the idea that many different environments and forces can exist together as a single system. This is one of the most important ideas within the Doctrine of

Balance, where differences are not considered weaknesses but necessary parts of a greater whole.

The flag was eventually officially adopted during the period when Solvyra was becoming a unified civilisation. Representatives from the eight islands gathered in the central settlement of Solvyra for a ceremony that would later become known as The Raising of the Banner.

The flag was raised for the first time after representatives agreed that its symbols represented the entire Solvyrian civilisation. The ceremony was important because the flag did not belong to one island, one god, one family or one political group. It belonged to everyone. From that point forward, the flag began appearing on government buildings, research facilities, temples, ships and important public ceremonies.

Over time, the flag became more than a political symbol. It became a representation of the

Solvyran worldview. The blue represents the sky and the unusual world in which they live.

The green represents life, nature and the civilisation they have created. The gold represents the Balance and the value of knowledge. The eagle represents Maradorn and the history of the Solvyran people, while the red bird represents Solvyra and its future. The crown represents the responsibility placed upon the people, while the overall arrangement of the symbols represents unity between different forces.

Today, the flag is one of the most important symbols in Solvyrian society. It is flown during religious ceremonies, national celebrations, scientific discoveries, military events and major moments in Solvyrian history. It is particularly important during ceremonies involving the

Eight Gods because the flag represents the people who have been entrusted with maintaining the Balance. The flag therefore acts as a constant reminder that Solvyra's technological achievements are not the only thing that defines its civilisation.

Ultimately, the Solvyrian flag represents the journey of an entire people. The eagle represents the world they came from, the red bird represents the civilisation they created, the green stripe represents the life and progress between them, the golden lines represent the Balance that holds everything together, and the blue sky represents the extraordinary world that became their home. Together, these symbols express the central philosophy of

Solvyrian civilisation: they came from the world below, rose into the sky, gained knowledge beyond what they had previously imagined, and were entrusted with protecting the Balance that allows their civilisation to exist. This fits directly with the existing Solvyrian belief that the gods shaped the Balance and the people were chosen to uphold it.
