Book VI · The City of Maradorn

City of Maradorn

Overview

The City of Maradorn is the capital and largest city of the country of Maradorn, located on the southernmost island of the Continent of Maradorn. It is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the known world and serves as the political, intellectual, cultural and economic centre of the Maradorian nation.

The city was originally inhabited by the Mythali, an ancient human-like civilisation known for its intelligence, wisdom and mysterious magical abilities. The first human explorers reached the region during the Aperion Era, with ancient Redwickian records documenting encounters with the Mythali in the region by Orbon 1003. Human settlement began soon afterwards, following the Mythali's eventual transfer of stewardship over the land.

The City of Maradorn is the largest city in the nation but does not possess an exceptionally high population density. The urban area is relatively spread out, with wide streets, open spaces, gardens, waterways and large areas of greenery separating many buildings and districts. It is largely self-sustainable but continues to import selected resources, including oil from Redwick and salt from Dunaria.

The city combines a largely medieval visual appearance with advanced knowledge and institutions. Traditional markets, stone buildings, temples and workshops exist alongside major educational institutions, scientific facilities and the national aerospace program. Computers and internet technology exist but remain rare and are largely limited to important institutions and high-level individuals.

Geography

The City of Maradorn is located on the southernmost island of the continent and occupies a distinctive U-shaped or banana-shaped landform. The main urban area is built within the sheltered hollow of the U, giving the city a naturally protected and recognisable geographical setting.

Behind the city rises the Mythalindor Mountain Range, which overlooks the capital and forms one of the most important natural and cultural features of the region.

Further surrounding the broader area are the Aelthorion Peaks, a vast mountain system containing several sacred mountains. The mountains, forests, fresh-water springs and surrounding jungle strongly influence the city's climate, culture, resources and religious traditions.

Maradorn is surrounded by extensive jungle. Rather than allowing uncontrolled development or logging, the government has placed strong restrictions on the destruction of natural forest.
