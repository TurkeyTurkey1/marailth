Book XXXI · The Eastern Islands

The Eastern Island Alliance

The Eastern Island Alliance is the oldest military and commercial league in the world, and the least understood by the nations outside it. Its members are Redwick, Cherryfall, Karhiem, Fungladom and Corvium. Solvyra lies on the same great island continent and is deliberately not a member; the Solvyrans have never asked, and the alliance has never offered.

It began as the Eastern Maritime League in Orbon 2800, when Redwick, Cherryfall and Karhiem agreed to cooperate in protecting the trade routes that fed all three of them. The League worked. It worked so well that over the following millennia it absorbed the smaller island powers, survived the collapse of several of its members' governments, and eventually became the coalition that fought the Great Diverse War — and then the permanent alliance that replaced it.

The alliance is not a government and has no standing army of its own. Each member keeps its crown, senate or council, and each keeps its own fleet. What the alliance provides is a promise: that the sea lanes of the eastern continent will be defended by all of them together, that no member will make a separate peace, and that no outside power will be permitted to establish a base among the islands. In practice this has held for three thousand Orbons, which is longer than most nations last.
