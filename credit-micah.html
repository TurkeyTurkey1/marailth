The hands behind the world

Micah

Creator of Redwick & Lywra · Co-Developer of the World

Chief Chronicler of Redwick

All credits

Responsible for the complete creation and development of Redwick and its associated lore, including the character of Lywra. Plays a major role in the wider worldbuilding of Maralith, contributing to the development of numerous nations, regions and their histories.

Key Contributions

Key contributions include the development of Karhiem, Fungladom and Aluven, with a particular focus on Aluven's history and wartime lore. Also contributes significantly to the lore of Sangar, especially its military and wartime history, alongside the development of Blushwood.

Serves as a leader and primary developer of Eiqias, overseeing its worldbuilding, lore and unique magic system. Responsible for creating the official map of Eiqias, as well as designing the flags and crests of all countries developed under their contribution.

Beyond geographical and historical worldbuilding, plays an important role in establishing and developing sports throughout Maralith, helping expand the world's cultures, traditions and recreational activities.

Character Representation — Marcus Aurelin

Also represented within the world through the Aurelin family line, most notably the character Marcus Aurelin, connecting their creative contributions to the wider narrative and character history of Maralith.
