World Creator & Primary Developer – Solvyra

Chief Chronicler of Solvyra

All credits

Responsible for the original creation and extensive development of Solvyra, overseeing the establishment of its identity, geographical foundations, historical background, cultures, political structures and wider lore. As one of the primary contributors to the world's development, plays a significant role in shaping Solvyra into a fully established region with its own distinct history, identity and place within the wider fictional setting.

Primary Creations & Developments

Solvyra

  • Original creator and primary developer of Solvyra.
  • Responsible for developing the majority of its established history, lore and geographical foundations.
  • Established its cultural identity, regional characteristics and broader world-building.
  • Developed its historical narratives, important events and connections to the wider world.
  • Responsible for shaping Solvyra's overall identity and ensuring its place within the world's expanding setting.

Stoomes

  • Independent creator and sole developer of Stoomes.
  • Responsible for its entire development, from its initial concept to its established identity.
  • Developed its geographical foundations, history, lore and defining characteristics.
  • Established its cultural and historical significance within the wider world.
  • Independently constructed its foundations and established its place within the broader fictional setting.

World-Building & Collaborative Development

Beyond individual regional development, plays an important role in the wider construction and expansion of the fictional world, collaborating with other creators and developers to establish a connected and cohesive setting. Responsibilities and contributions include:

  • Contributing to the broader development of the world's history, geography and overarching lore.
  • Collaborating on interconnected stories, historical events and narratives between different regions.
  • Helping establish relationships, connections and shared histories between nations and territories.
  • Contributing ideas and creative direction to the continued expansion of the world.
  • Supporting other developers in maintaining consistency across the world's established lore.
  • Helping shape the overall identity and direction of the wider fictional setting.

Cartography, Mapping & Digital Development

Also responsible for the technical and visual presentation of the world through the creation and development of supporting digital resources. This includes:

  • Designing and developing geographical maps that represent the world's continents, countries, territories and important locations.
  • Establishing and refining regional boundaries, geographical layouts and cartographic details.
  • Developing websites to document, organise and showcase the world's expanding lore and geographical information.
  • Creating accessible digital platforms through which the world, its history and its various regions can be explored.
  • Supporting the organisation, presentation and preservation of the world's growing collection of information.

Overall Responsibilities

Plays a significant role in both the independent development of individual regions and the collaborative expansion of the wider fictional world. Through the creation of Solvyra and Stoomes, contributions to overarching world-building, and the development of maps and websites, helps establish the foundations, structure and presentation of the world as a whole.
