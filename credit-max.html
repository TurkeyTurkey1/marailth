Max

World Creator & Primary Lore Developer

Chief Chronicler of Maradorn

All credits

The primary creator and architect of the world, responsible for establishing its foundations, developing its geography, constructing its nations, and creating the extensive lore and histories that connect its many regions. As the original creator of the world, he oversees the broader world-building process, ensuring consistency, continuity, and depth across its continents, countries, cultures, and histories.

Primary Creations & Developments

Maradorn

  • Original creator of Maradorn and its entire established lore.
  • Responsible for its history, geographical development, political structure, cultures, and broader world significance.
  • Creator of the Plains of Untail, Point of Eloke, and Nerava.

Collaborative Development

Alongside his independent creations, he has contributed to several other major regions and projects:

  • Solvyra — Assistant developer.
  • Ellariondale — Assistant developer.
  • Aluven, Sangar, Shadowfen, Ebonwood and Corvium — Primary development and lore contributions.

World-Building Responsibilities

Beyond individual countries and geographical regions, his most significant responsibility is the development of the world's overarching lore and structure. This includes:

  • Establishing the world's foundations, geography, and overall design.
  • Developing interconnected histories, events, and historical narratives.
  • Establishing relationships and connections between nations and regions.
  • Maintaining consistency throughout the world's expanding lore.
  • Creating the foundations upon which other developers can build their own regions.
  • Overseeing the broader direction and continuity of the fictional world.

Character Representation — Max

Max has developed Maxorian, a character who represents his influence within the fictional world. Through Maxorian, Max contributes to the world's development and its narrative, with the character sharing many conceptual similarities with his creator. Maxorian serves as a representation of Max's ideas and presence within the world rather than simply being an independent character.
